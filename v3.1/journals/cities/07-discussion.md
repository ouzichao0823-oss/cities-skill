# Cities 讨论节写作指南（真实语料版）

> **数据基础**：Zotero 库内真实 Cities 论文"讨论 Discussion"节精提取 数十篇。全部例句逐字摘录自原文，可回库核实。
> **[DATE] 重写原则（改写说明，推翻 v1 的模板化写法）**：
> 1. 写作逻辑只能从 Zotero 库真实 Cities 论文提炼；**你自己的论文是被指导对象，不是语料来源**。
> 2. 不是罗列"多少篇/百分比/多少条句式"，而是提炼"讨论要回答什么、与文献怎么对话、机制怎么解释、政策怎么说、局限怎么写"等真正有用的写作判断，配中英例子，让英文不好的模型照骨架写出合格英文。
> **v1 遗留问题（已修正）**：①旧版把"空间尺度辩护/大数据偏差坦白"当城市研究讨论的签名，实测只覆盖少数论文，多数讨论根本没有这些块；②旧版漏了最常见形态——**"Discussion and conclusion"合并节**（数十篇里无独立讨论节、讨论并入结论/结果节的论文占大半），也漏了文献对话的 contrary / extends 两类；③旧版口号化，没有给出"矛盾结果怎么处置""null 结果怎么写"这类真实高频难题的答案。

## 〇、这一章的"工作"是什么

读者读完讨论节，要能回答四个问题：
1. **发现意味着什么**——把统计结果翻译成机制故事（为什么会这样，不是又报一遍结果）；
2. **与已有文献是印证还是矛盾**——逐条对照，一致给证据，矛盾给原因，空缺给补充；
3. **对城市实践有什么用**——政策含义：点名行动者（planners/policy makers/decision-makers）+ 具体动作 + 适用边界；
4. **局限是什么**——承认数据/方法/外推的边界，并指向未来研究。

**判断标准**：讨论是结果的**转译**，不是结果的**复述**。真实论文的讨论里找不到"我们发现了 X，然后 Y，然后 Z"式流水账，只找得到"为什么是 X、X 与谁呼应、X 该怎么办、X 不能推广到哪"。

## 一、真实论文怎么组织（数十篇实测）

### 1.1 先判断：独立 Discussion 节，还是并入结论/结果节

数十篇里**无独立讨论节、讨论并入其他节的占大半**，这是 Cities 的常态，不是缺陷：

| 形态 | 特征 | 代表论文 |
|---|---|---|
| **合并节**（Conclusion and discussion / Discussion and conclusions / Discussion and implications） | 讨论功能由合并节前几段承担，常见于"结果自解释性强 + 篇幅紧凑"的论文 | 2015_Zhou、2016_Tammaru（1.4 Summary and discussion）、2019_Zhang、2020_Wang、2021_Choi、2021_Lu、2022_Hu、2022_Gao、2023_Qiao、2023_Tao、2024_Chen、2024_Zhang、2026_Ding、2026_Li_8809、2026_Zhuang、Ta2021、Tao2020 |
| **结果节内嵌**（边报告边对话） | 每个发现后紧跟"与文献对照 + 机制解释 + 含义"，适合变量多的短文，避免单独讨论节重复 | 2014_VanHolm、2017__6166、2021_Poku-Boansi、2023_Shi、2024_Sun、2024_Aguilera-García、2026_Guo、Toger2023、Vitrano2023、AllenFarber2020 |
| **独立 Discussion 节**（含小标题/小节） | 讨论功能多（机制+对话+政策+局限都要展开）或每个发现都需深谈时使用 | 2014_Wu（四小标题）、2017_Tan（4.4）、2022_Li、2023_Liu、2025_Chen、2026_He、2026_Won、Gao2025、Kim2024、DongMiller2026、2026_Fan、2026_Liu_7724、2026_Liu_8807、2026_Wang_8274、2025_Zhou、2026_Li_8817、2025_Azari、2025_Cao、2026_Chamberlain、2026_Lee、Zheng2026 |

**判断逻辑**：你的结果节是否已经把"机制/文献/含义"说透了？说透了 → 合并节收尾即可；结果节只报数字、解释欠账多 → 独立 Discussion。聚类+活动空间类稿件若结果节只有聚类与统计结果，则讨论应独立成节或做 Discussion and implications，给机制与政策留足篇幅。

### 1.2 功能块与真实顺序变体

六个功能块：**综合发现 → 机制解释 → 文献对话 → 政策含义 → 局限 → 未来**。真实论文的顺序变体：

- **经典顺序**（发现→机制→对话→政策→局限）：2022_Hu、Kim2024、DongMiller2026、2025_Chen、Tao2020、Ta2021。
- **先立贡献再对话**（贡献/方法定位 → 逐条对话 → 政策+泼冷水）：2022_Gao（5 段：方法定位→个体发现对话→空间格局对话→机制解释→政策+反向警示）；2025_Azari（5.1 方法贡献，5.2 政策含义）；2026_Ding（机制→贡献→局限三段论）。
- **对话先行**（回望文献缺口 → 发现逐条挂文献 → 政策）：2023_Liu（5.1 Key findings 用 First/Second/Third，每条"发现→一致性→差异性→归因"）；2026_Liu_8807（从"挑战假设"切入）。
- **机制先行**：Gao2025（小标题"从心理学视角解释空间差异原因"→ 政策一句 → 局限独立小节）。
- **理论收束型**：2014_Wu（形态→机制→新发现→理论整合四个小标题）。
- **政策专章型**（结果内嵌对话+机制，政策独立成节）：2022_Zhang（Section 5 Policy and practice implications）、2026_Guo（第 6 章 Policy implication）、Zheng2026（5.3）、2017__6166（第 6 节）。
- **局限独立节/小节**：2026_He（4.3）、2026_Liu_7724（5.3）、Gao2025（Limitations and Future Work）、2026_Won（5.4）、2025_Chen（6.1.3）、DongMiller2026（Limitations 独立成节）。

**要点**：功能块可以换序、可以合并（如"政策含义内嵌于机制解释"，2026_Lee；"局限在 6.1 末"，2026_Liu_8807），但**五个功能块在全文必须都出现**——缺了"局限"，审稿人视为过度宣称；缺了"政策"，城市研究讨论被视为没落地。

### 1.3 合并节"Discussion and conclusion"的处理

合并节最常见的排布（按真实论文）：**发现总结（1–2 段）→ 机制/对话（主体）→ 政策含义（1 段或分条）→ 局限未来（1–2 段）**，最后一段回扣贡献/框架。真实例证：
- 2022_Hu：重述定位（1 段）→ 总结发现+机制（3 段）→ 对话+情境反思（1 段）→ 政策（1 段，按性别分层）→ 局限未来（1 段）。
- 2023_Tao：总结主要发现 → 与文献对话 → 机制解释 → 政策含义。
- 2026_Zhuang：6.1 Major findings（重述缺口→发现+机制→对话）→ 6.2 Policy implications（宏观分层+微观分类）→ 6.3 Limitations（三点）。
- 2019_Zhang：合并节 P2–P5 承担讨论功能（机制→对话→政策），P1 与末段承担结论。

**注意**：合并节里"讨论在前、结论在后"（Discussion and conclusion / Conclusion and discussion 两种题名都常见）；无论哪种，**结论成分只占 1–2 段**，主体必须是讨论。

### 1.4 讨论开头的衔接（结果最后一个发现 → 讨论第一个论点）

真实论文的六种开头桥（避免讨论凭空起头）：

- **任务声明式**（回顾方法贡献 + 声明本节任务）：2017_Tan——`The geovisualization... revealed... The descriptive statistical analysis led to... While the structural equation modelling further testified... We provide further interpretation of these results as follows.`
- **总览句**（一段给全部讨论锚点）：2026_He——`Our findings reveal the dynamic evolution of airspace resources with altitude and underscore the critical role of urban morphology in low-altitude airspace management...`
- **总起回扣**（This study sheds light on... + 回扣摘要承诺）：DongMiller2026——`This study sheds light on the distinct out-of-home non-work/school activity participation patterns by emergent hybrid workers...`
- **重述缺口+方案回扣**（先"已有研究缺什么"再"本文做了什么"）：2026_Zhuang——`Existing studies have largely examined... In contrast, comparatively limited attention has been paid to...`；Tao2020——`Although considerable research has shed light on [议题], the unique role of [关键因素] on [结果] over time has not been fully examined. This study has addressed such a gap by [核心动作].`
- **挑战假设切入**（文献假设 → 本文证据推翻）：2026_Liu_8807——`However, our findings challenge this assumption, showing that low-mobility groups' needs for non-ubiquitous urban functions and services are similar to those of high-mobility groups.`
- **方法遗产回访**（从经典研究说起 → 指出未进展）：2026_Won——`A seminal study by Appleyard and Lintell (1972) showed that... Methods for capturing human behavior have advanced little beyond Appleyard and Lintell's original approach.`

**判断标准**：讨论第一段必须回答"我们凭什么讨论"——要么回扣文献缺口，要么回扣结果承诺，要么回扣方法贡献；不能从第一个发现直接开始。

## 二、段落级写法（逐段功能）

| 段落 | 功能 | 常见内容 | 真实例证 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **第 1 段：衔接/总起** | 从结果过渡到讨论，立讨论任务 | 任务声明、总览句、缺口回扣、挑战假设 | 2017_Tan、2026_He、2026_Zhuang、2026_Liu_8807 |
| **主体段：机制解释** | 回答"为什么"，每个发现配机制 | may be because / attributed to / due to / On one hand...On the other hand | 2015_Zhou、2022_Hu、Zheng2026、2026_Fan、Gao2025 |
| **主体段：文献对话** | 回答"与谁呼应/与谁矛盾"，每条带引用 | consistent with / contrary to / extends / complements | 2014_VanHolm、2021_Poku-Boansi、2023_Qiao、2025_Chen |
| **政策段** | 落地：行动者+动作+边界 | To address...it is recommended to... / call for policymakers / For planners | 2024_Chen、2023_Liu、2022_Zhang、Zheng2026 |
| **局限段** | 诚实边界+未来方向 | There were some limitations... First/Second/Third | 2026_Liu_7724、2026_Fan、Gao2025、AllenFarber2020 |

**段内推进模式**：真实论文的讨论段几乎都是"结果复述（一句）→ 机制/文献（主体）→ 含义/限定（收束）"三段式；段与段之间用 First/Second/Third、On one hand...On the other hand、In contrast、Nevertheless 等显式连接词。

## 三、句式与例子（真实提取，按用途取用，附出处）

### 3.1 与文献对话（一）：印证 corroborates / consistent with / in line with

- `This finding corroborates the findings of <author> who argued that <claim>.`（2021_Poku-Boansi_7965："This finding where most of the respondents travel longer distances to work corroborates the findings of Cervero and Day (2008) who argued that people staying in sub-urban and peri-urban areas travel longer distances to access social services."）
- `These findings are consistent with that of <author> who report that <claim>.`（2016_Wang_8603："These findings are consistent with that of Krivo et al. (2013) who report that there is a positive association between segregation in residential neighborhoods and in the neighborhoods where daily activities are conducted."）
- `The <finding> is in line with the conclusions of related studies that <普适论断> (<cites>).`（2024_Sun_7832："This finding is also in line with the conclusions of related studies that disadvantaged groups in cities tend to be more likely to face higher levels of segregation (Hu et al., 2022; Östh et al., 2018; Tao et al., 2020)."）
- `This is aligned with most studies (<cites>) where <X> mainly influences <Y> indirectly through <mechanism>.`（2022_Hu_7955："This is aligned with most studies (Clark et al., 2020; Hilbrecht et al., 2014; Sun et al., 2020) where commute time mainly influences life satisfaction indirectly through time use and the level of satisfaction with those activities."）
- `These findings are consistent with the <理论> introduced earlier in this paper. <理论机制句>.`（2021_Jang_7876："These findings are consistent with the bid-rent theory introduced earlier in this paper. Areas with favourable access impose high rents, and high-income households that can pay high rents occupy such areas."）
- `This finding is consistent with international literature at the <尺度> of <地区> (<cite>).`（2023_Shi_7664："This finding is consistent with international literature at the microscale of Nordic cities (Næss, 2012)."）
- 中文说明：印证句的动词池是 corroborates / is consistent with / is in line with / is aligned with / is consistent with the conclusions of；后面必须跟"谁 + 发现什么"（作者+年份+结论），不能只写"与已有研究一致"。

### 3.2 与文献对话（二）：矛盾 contrary to / in contrast / diverge（+ 矛盾处置）

- `This finding is also contrary to the findings of <author> who found that <claim>.`（2021_Poku-Boansi_7965："This finding is also contrary to the findings of Poumanyvong et al. (2012) who found that in developing regions and countries, walking, cycling and mass transit are the main modes of travel."）
- `These results diverge from previous findings based on <对照方法> (<cites>) in that <核心分歧>.`（2016_Tammaru_8475："These results diverge from previous findings based on cross-sectional data (e.g. Ellis et al., 2004; Marcinczak et al., 2015) in that the link between residential segregation and workplace segregation seems much weaker and even non-existent for immigrant men arriving from Global South."）
- `This finding contradicts to the findings of <author> where they show <对比发现>.`（Toger2023："This finding contradicts to the findings of Mooses et al. (2016) where they show a significant variation across ethnic origins in Estonia."）
- `This contrasts with earlier findings suggesting <旧结论> (<cite>), highlighting the influence of <地方因素> and the <文化差异>.`（2025_Chen_7515："This contrasts with earlier findings suggesting male dominance in urban core (Fainstein & Servon, 2005), highlighting the influence of Zhuhai's specific industrial structure and the cultural variations in gender segregation patterns."）
- 语境化反驳（不显著/反常结果）：2026_Li_8817——`Interestingly, the proximity to a police station has insignificant RAT for both genders, contrary to many Western studies (Roper, 2016). ... This finding demonstrates that urban studies cannot be "copied and pasted".`
- **矛盾后的处置（必做，三类）**：
  - 归因于方法差异：2023_Oostenbach_7984——`Possible explanations for these differences in findings may be that Kerr et al. (2012) only compared workers to non-workers, whereas we compared multiple work hours groups...`
  - 归因于混淆因素：Tao2020——`These findings are in part contradictory with some previous research that found modest disparity in daily mobility between the low-income and other groups, wherein confounding factors were not explicitly controlled for in the comparison (e.g., Wang and Li, 2016).`
  - 承认差异但强调模式一致：2026_He_8940——`Although the absolute thresholds differ from those reported in previous studies (Cho & Yoon, 2018; Oh & Yoon, 2024), the overall pattern remains consistent.`

### 3.3 与文献对话（三）：拓展/细化 extends / goes beyond / complements / supplements

- `Drawing on the <concept> notion, this study used <dimensions> to quantify <X>, extending the measurement of <Y>.`（2022_Gao_7829："Drawing on the activity space notion, this study used the extensity of activity space, the diversity of activities and travel efficiency to quantify the access to opportunities, extending the measurement of social exclusion."）
- `This finding complements the discussions on the relation between <A>, <B> and <C> (<cite>).`（2023_Qiao_5967："This finding complements the discussions on the relation between precarity, insecurity and platform dependence (Schor et al., 2020)."）
- `While <author> (<year>) suggested that <旧结论>, our findings reveal a more nuanced pattern: <新结论>.`（2025_Chen_7515："While Gauvin et al. (2020) suggested that men's travel routes are more varied and women's are more uniform, our findings reveal a more nuanced pattern: women's mobility is both diverse and concentrated, whereas men's movements, though more singular in purpose, are more spatially dispersed."）
- `Existing studies mostly use <静态指标> to describe <pattern> (ref). In contrast, the <measure> in this study is built on <actual data>, providing a more direct measure of <concept>.`（2026_Wang_8274："Existing studies mostly use static indicators such as facility density and street-network connectivity to describe the center–periphery gradient (Wu & Niu, 2019). In contrast, the LMI in this study is built on residents' actual activity trajectories and incorporates their real use of neighborhood facilities..."）
- `However, our findings challenge this assumption, showing that <发现>.`（2026_Liu_8807："However, our findings challenge this assumption, showing that low-mobility groups' needs for non-ubiquitous urban functions and services are similar to those of high-mobility groups."）
- `This responds directly to calls in the literature: for example, <作者> stress that <引用观点> and advocate <做法> rather than studying each in isolation.`（2026_Ding_7711："This responds directly to calls in the literature: for example, Liu et al. (2019) stress that interactions of spatial and social dimensions have 'both conceptual and practical significance' and advocate combining them rather than studying each in isolation."）
- 新发现声明：2014_Wu_6549——`Compared to existing case studies of Chinese cities this analysis is the first to apply principal component analysis for socio-spatial structure analysis at the community level.`
- 中文说明：拓展句的动词池是 extends / complements / supplements / challenges / provides a more direct measure / reveals a more nuanced pattern；作用是"本文在文献坐标系里占住一个增量位置"——可仿 2026_Wang 的"In contrast, the <measure> in this study is built on..."句式与静态指标文献划界。

### 3.4 机制解释句式（为什么会出现这个结果）

- `Our finding of <结果> amongst <组> may be because <机制解释>.`（2023_Oostenbach_7984："Our finding of less frequent visits to their primary supermarket amongst those working up to full-time and those working overtime may be because workers plan major regular shopping trips to be performed on a preferred day and at preferred intervals (Nordfalt, 2009), to adapt to their time availability."）
- `This discrepancy may be attributed to <机制>, which leads <对象> to <行为1> and <行为2> (<cite>).`（Zheng2026："This discrepancy may be attributed to energy depletion caused by long working hours, which leads residents to prefer low-energy near-domain leisure and actively avoid complex activities requiring cross-regional travel (Cook & Gazmararian, 2018)."）
- `This is most likely caused by a combination of <原因A> and <原因B>.`（2016_Tammaru_8475："This is most likely caused by a combination of finding jobs through migrant networks and facing difficulties in matching their skills—which were obtained in a very different context—to jobs in the Swedish labor market."）
- `This finding may indicate that <mechanism>.`（2024_Aguilera-García_3062："This finding may indicate that this transport mode is perceived as green mobility only among frequent users."）
- 结构性机制对照：2026_Fan_8053——`On one hand, spatial factors such as locational attributes and accessibility to transport infrastructure constitute broadly shared constraints across demographic groups, reflecting the structural constraints of urban spatial resources on mobility. On the other hand, complex non-linear interactions between the built environment and individual social attributes and resource endowments shape differentiated responses...`
- 制度机制：2015_Zhou_7343——`China's unique historic path of the danwei system and the series of policy reforms during the period of economic transformation are the invisible hand reshaping both the built environment and social space in urban China and deeply impact on individuals' out-of-home activities.`
- 反直觉结果的情境解释：2022_Hu_7955——`The non-significant impact of the commute time on commute satisfaction may be related to the short commute time and light traffic volume in local areas. In our sample, the average commute time for one trip is approximately 12.3 min for women and 14.9 min for men, which could hardly harm commute satisfaction in the context of smaller Chinese cities...`
- 机制图收编：2026_Ding_7711——`The driving mechanism behind the formation of residential spatial differentiation is summarized in Fig. 5.`
- 意象/隐喻升华：Zheng2026——`This indicates that long working hours may marginalize social interactions and recreation, as individuals are more likely to fall into a 'work-home pendulum'.`
- 中文说明：机制句的动词池是 may be because / may be attributed to / is most likely caused by / may indicate that / could encourage / is closely tied to；机制解释不是猜谜，要落在**可命名的机制**（时间替代、能量消耗、制度路径、空间结构、家庭分工）上，并可带引用支撑（2023_Oostenbach、Gao2025 用文献链拼出中介链条）。

### 3.5 政策建议句式（对城市管理者/规划者的建议）

- `To address <问题> and promote <目标>, it is recommended to implement <政策>.`（2024_Chen_5974："To address social inequality in age disparities and promote social mobility and integration, it is recommended to implement affordable housing policies near workplaces."）
- `Noting that <群体> have <特征> and a strong reliance on <设施>, promoting <政策A> and improving <政策B> may greatly improve their situations.`（2019_Zhang_7717："Noting that the residents of affordable housing have the longest commuting distance among the four groups and a strong reliance on public transportation, promoting jobs-housing balance and improving the public transit system may greatly improve their situations."）
- `Due to <模式>, focusing <投资> on <区域> may be more effective.`（2017__6166："Due to the suburbanized job and residential distributions of non-local workers, focusing public transport investments on the suburban areas may be more effective."）
- `These empirical findings call for policymakers to reconsider whether <政策> adequately reflects <现实> or merely represents <批评>.`（2023_Liu_8071："These empirical findings call for policymakers to reconsider whether administrative planning during urban development adequately reflects spatial interaction patterns or merely represents a top-down imposition."）
- `These findings suggest that to improve <目标>, <政策主体> should support <政策建议>.`（Ta2021："These findings suggest that to improve the well-being and social integration of migrants, social policies should support the compact neighborhood with high density and equitable distribution of urban opportunities."）
- 双向政策（On the one hand...On the other hand）：2021_Cao_8109——`On the one hand, it is necessary to build additional public transport and public service facilities to encourage diversified travels in suburban or outskirt areas. On the other hand, to avoid the partial congestion caused by the extreme attraction of urban hubs, when planning the establishment of urban infrastructures, policy makers should fully consider how to retain the hierarchical and polycentric structure of urban mobility.`
- 按读者对象分组：Zheng2026——`For industrial development planners, <发现> suggest <政策含义>. In addition, <发现> can also serve as an evaluation tool to <目的>.`
- 优先级句：2026_Wang_8274——`Under resource constraints, priority should be given to <first action>, followed by <second action>, to more effectively promote <outcome>.`
- 建议+副作用承认（显得平衡）：2022_Zhang_7714——`One suggestion associated with this finding is to relax the restriction of free bus eligibility, for example, relax the age restriction to 60 years old. Of course, this relaxation might cause other problems, such as increasing government finance pressure, increasing congestions in public transports, etc.`
- 政策设防（防止被误读）：2026_Li_8809——`These insights are policy-relevant, but not in the sense of overriding public-health goals during a pandemic, when reducing contact and suppressing mixing may be necessary.`
- 反向警示/泼冷水：2022_Gao_7829——`Although this study demonstrated the advantages of cars in facilitating activity participation, providing private vehicles for the people living in socially excluded areas is not encouraged.`
- 中文说明：政策句必须有**行动者**（planners / policy makers / decision-makers / the Korean government）+ **具体动作**（implement/build/focus/promote/relax）+ **对象**（区域/人群/设施）；可加优先级、副作用、设防使建议可信。通勤/规划类政策含义可仿 2019_Zhang（jobs-housing balance + public transit）与 2017__6166（按空间分布聚焦投资）。

### 3.6 局限与未来句式（limitations 段的标准写法）

- 标准起头：2023_Oostenbach_7984——`There were some limitations to this study. Firstly, no data were collected on participant work schedules.`；2026_Fan_8053——`However, this research has several limitations. First, the built-environment indicators primarily capture static spatial attributes, offering limited insight into the dynamic adaptive behaviors elicited by environmental shifts or extreme events.`；Gao2025——`This study had several limitations. First, it focused solely on outdoor leisure activities on weekends and did not consider activities during non-working hours on weekdays.`
- 每条局限 = 问题 + 影响：2026_Liu_7724——`Despite its strengths, the proposed framework has several limitations that merit attention. First, demographic bias is a significant concern. Mobile phone data often underrepresents older adults and children due to lower smartphone usage.`；2026_Liu_8807——`This study acknowledges several limitations that suggest directions for future research. First, ... Second, ... Third, ... Finally, ...`
- 因果局限：AllenFarber2020——`The cross-sectional design of our study also limits our ability to conclude about causal relations, and brings into question the validity of our method for calculating effect sizes.`
- 外推限制：2026_Chamberlain_8947——`An important consideration when interpreting the results of this study is that the selected region is not reflective of many older, denser, or more infrastructurally complex metropolitan areas such as Lisbon, London, Beijing, Rio de Janeiro, and New York City.`
- 可推广性声明（先限定案例再谈普适）：2026_He_8940——`Although this study was conducted in Shenzhen, the exposure risk and accessibility index models developed herein possess a high degree of generalizability and can be readily applied to low-altitude airspace assessments in other urban areas.`
- 防过度声明：Tao2020——`While we cannot assert that the low-income population in Hong Kong are socially excluded and segregated from others, the hazard of social exclusion for this group, based on the current findings, is certainly not negligible.`；Vitrano2023——`However, time wealth is only one dimension of spatiotemporal accessibility.`
- 未来方向句：Tao2020——`For future research, it would be worthwhile to <方向>, which will provide <更优结果>.`；2026_He（每条局限后紧跟未来方向）；2026_Liu_7724（按技术层/数据层分列未来方向）。
- 中文说明：局限段的标准写法是编号列点（First/Second/Third/Finally），每条"问题→影响→（可选）未来方向"；语气是技术性的诚实，不是道歉。

### 3.7 开头衔接句

- 任务声明式：2017_Tan——`The <方法A> revealed <发现1>, and also shed light on <机制线索>. ... We provide further interpretation of these results as follows.`
- 总览句：2026_He——`Our findings reveal <发现> and underscore the critical role of <要素> in <领域>.`
- 缺口回扣：Tao2020——`Although considerable research has shed light on [议题], the unique role of [关键因素] on [结果] over time has not been fully examined. This study has addressed such a gap by [核心动作].`
- 挑战假设：2026_Liu_8807——`However, our findings challenge this assumption, showing that <发现>.`

## 四、陷阱与红线（真实审稿/批注教训）

1. **❌ 讨论重复结果**：把 results 的数字再报一遍。真实论文的讨论是转译（机制/对话/政策），不是复述；2024_Sun 明说"结果即讨论"的结构就是为了避免重复。
2. **❌ 文献对话只报喜不报忧**：通篇 is consistent with，遇到矛盾/null 结果就跳过。真实论文对 null 结果给完整篇幅（Kim2024 的 walkability、2026_Lee 的公园、2022_Hu 的通勤满意度、2026_Guo 的争议结果），并用情境/测量局限解释。
3. **❌ 政策建议无行动者/无动作**：空喊"应当重视交通问题"。真实政策句都有行动者（planners/policy makers/decision-makers）+ 具体动作 + 对象，必要时带副作用承认（2022_Zhang）与设防（2026_Li_8809）。
4. **❌ 局限写得太谦虚或太敷衍**：不写局限=过度宣称；写"由于作者水平有限"=不专业。标准写法是编号列点、每条=问题+影响+未来方向（2026_Liu_7724、2026_Liu_8807、2026_He）。
5. **❌ 矛盾结果不给归因**：与文献矛盾必须解释"为什么不同"（方法/数据/情境/混淆因素），见 3.2 的三类处置；只报矛盾不解释=暴露方法漏洞。
6. **❌ 政策建议与本文指标脱钩**：建议要能从自己的表格/系数推导出来（2022_Zhang 从 Table 6 弹性出发、Zheng2026 从加班指标出发）。
7. **❌ 强结论不加设防**：要做"不能断言/只是维度之一/不推广到其他情境"的限定（Tao2020、Vitrano2023、2026_Chamberlain），否则审稿人挑过度声称。
8. **❌ 讨论凭空起头**：没有衔接（任务声明/总览/缺口回扣/挑战假设），直接从第一个发现开始（见 1.4）。
9. **❌ 机制解释停在统计关联**：只写"X 与 Y 显著相关"，不解释为什么；机制句要有可命名的机制（时间替代、能量消耗、制度路径、空间结构、家庭分工），可带引用支撑。
10. **❌ 元统计堆砌**：不写"数十篇里 26 篇合并节"这种话（本指南仅供写作参考，论文正文绝不出现元统计）。
