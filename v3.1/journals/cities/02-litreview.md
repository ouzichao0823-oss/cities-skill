## 〇、这一章的"工作"是什么

读者读完文献综述，要能回答三个问题：
1. **这个领域研究到什么程度了**（哪些结论已牢固确立，证据链长什么样）；
2. **还缺什么**（缺口具体在哪个环节，不是"研究不足"这种空话）；
3. **为什么本文补这个缺口是合理的**（不是为创新而创新，而是顺着领域逻辑自然推出的）。

**判断标准**：综述不是文献的流水账（谁研究了什么、按年份排列），而是一条**论证链**——每一段的主题句都在推进这条链，链的终点是 gap。

## 一、综述的组织逻辑（真实论文怎么排）

### 1.1 三条组织逻辑（实测 数十篇，按出现频率）

从真实语料看，综述的组织逻辑不是"罗列文献"，而是**用一两条主线把文献串起来**。数十篇里最常见的三种：

| 组织逻辑 | 特征 | 代表论文 |
|---|---|---|
| **① 主题线索递进（最多）** | 2-3 条主题线索，按"概念→谱系→现状→缺口"层层收窄，与引言漏斗同构；综述是引言的放大版 | Zheng 2026（加班危害→工具→群体空白）、Hu 2022（个体→家庭→小城市）、Kim 2024（界定→福祉→直接测量工具）、Ta 2021（概念与测量→机制→中国情境） |
| **② 两股研究流互补** | 先分述两条研究传统各自的成就与局限，再指出"只有极少数研究尝试结合"或"应互补" | Wang & Li 2016（暴露维度流 vs 活动空间特征流）、Palm 2025（mobilities of care + social exclusion）、Toger 2023（休闲移动性文献 vs 交通分析文献） |
| **③ 谱系—缺口—焦点** | 先讲领域成熟谱系（学派/方法演进），再指出空白，最后聚焦本文补位 | Shi & Yeh 2023（社会区分析谱系→geodemography→mesoscale 空白）、Gao 2025（数据源演进→因素主题并列→双缺口）、Zhang 2019（居住隔离谱系→AAS 现状→PAS 理论工具→双缺口） |

**关键发现**：
- **综述与引言同构**：Zheng 2026 / Ta 2021 等论文的综述节与引言是同一漏斗的放大版——引言点到为止，综述给出概念谱系 + 理论工具 + 逐篇证据。
- **主线通常是"概念/方法演进"或"尺度收窄"**，不是时间线。2026_Liu_7724 明确"按数据类型/方法类型组织，非按时间"。
- **结尾必须落在 gap**：最后一段（或最后小节）把前面的线索合并成一个或两个具体缺口（见第三节）。

### 1.2 综述在文中的两种位置形态（实测）

| 形态 | 特征 | 代表论文 |
|---|---|---|
| **独立综述节**（Section 2 Literature review / related work） | 2-3 小节，每节一条主线；篇幅可占全文 1/3 | 2019_Zhang（8 段）、Ta 2021（2.1-2.3）、Hu 2022（2.1-2.3）、Zheng 2026（2.1-2.3）、Xiao 2020（2.1-2.3） |
| **嵌入引言**（引言内 2-4 段完成综述） | 综述功能由引言段落承担；无独立节 | DongMiller 2026、AllenFarber 2020（2.1/2.2 实为引言内小节）、Vitrano 2023（引言内 + 理论节） |

**判断标准**（来自 00-total.md 骨架 A/B）：你的研究是否需要**概念框架图/理论节**来支撑论证？需要 → 独立综述节 + 框架图；纯数据驱动实证 → 嵌入引言即可。

### 1.3 段内组织：证据链式推进（不是条目罗列）

真实论文综述段内的标准推进是：
```
主题句（论点）→ 概念/框架（谁定义的）→ 证据分层/列举（谁、用什么数据、发现什么）→ 转折/总结句（链推进到哪了）
```

实测代表（2019_Zhang 综述 P3，"居住视角失效"的实证群）：
> "For instance, Schnell and Yoav (2001) emphasized that ... Ellis et al. (2004) found ... Burgess, Wilson, and Lupton (2005) found ... Shareck, Kestens, and Frohlich (2014) found ..."

每条引用配**一句研究发现**，不是堆引用。这是"证据群引用"（example-led citation chain）——比"（A, 2019; B, 2020; C, 2021）"式堆引用更有说服力。

**句骨架（英文不好的模型直接套）**：
```
In measuring [construct], [Author] used [data] to capture [measure]. They found that [finding].
[Author], studying [context], showed that [finding]; [Author], in [context], found [finding].
For instance, [Author] ([year]) emphasized that ...; [Author] ([year]) found ...; [Author] ([year]) found ...
```

## 二、主题句怎么写（真实论文的三种主题句）

数十篇的主题句可归纳为三种功能，每段第一句必须说清"这段要论证什么"：

| 功能 | 句式特征 | 真实例证 |
|---|---|---|
| **下定义/总领** | 给出核心概念的定义或分类，带出处 | Zhang 2019："Segregation is a multi-dimensional phenomenon that involves the social, spatial, and temporal dimensions."；Hu 2022："Time is limited on weekdays and longer commutes suggest that less time is therefore available for other activities." |
| **报现状/报趋势** | "increasing number of studies / has received increased attention / 领域已进展到哪" | Zhang 2019："In the past few years, an increasing number of studies have argued that segregation studies should be extended conceptually from residential segregation to spatiotemporal segregation of social groups in activity space."；Hu 2022："As an important dimension of everyday life, subjective well-being (SWB) has received increased attention during recent decades." |
| **转折推进/报缺口** | 用 However / Yet / Nevertheless 推进到本段要论证的缺口 | Zheng 2026："However, there are still some limitations in the current research on overtime work: First, ..."；Hu 2022："However, most evidence is based on individual-level data; very little comes from the household level." |

**段内推进的常见连接词链**（真实出现）：In the past few years → However → Further → Therefore → In this study / This paper aims to...（2019_Zhang 引言 P1 内部）。

**主题句高频夹注多文献建立"共识"感**（2026_Lee_7708 实测）：主题句一次引 3-6 篇（如 5Ds 框架句一次引 5 篇），作用是让读者感到"这是领域共识，不是作者一家之言"。

## 三、综述末尾的 Gap 段（真实论文怎么写）

**核心结论（与 01-introduction.md 对齐）**：综述 gap 与引言 gap 是**同三种写法**——单线推进 / 枚举缺陷 / 极简一句。数十篇实测分布：枚举缺陷最多，单线推进次之，极简一句用于局部小节。

### 3.1 枚举缺陷式（综述主 gap 最常用）

总起句给出判断（"still at a nascent stage / two research gaps"）+ First/Second（或 On one hand/On the other）逐条枚举**具体**缺陷 + 每条落在"具体没做什么"。

**真实例证 1**（Ta 2021，总起 + First/Second）：
> "Nevertheless, the understanding of activity space-based segregation is still at a nascent stage. First, current research has largely focused on the size of activity spaces and the amount of time spent on different activities among different social groups (Wang & Li, 2016; Zhang et al., 2019). However, there is a lack of a comprehensive evaluation of the variety in activity participation that meets migrants' different daily needs. Second, there are no consistent results on how spatial disadvantages in neighborhood environment are related to segregation in activity space."

**真实例证 2**（Gao 2025，先总起"two limitations"再逐条展开，每条=缺陷陈述+重要性论证+文献支撑）：
> "However, the studies reported in recent literature have two limitations. First, in only a few studies have working hours been considered as a factor that affects the likelihood of participating in outdoor leisure activities. Working hours are widely regarded as crucial factors in research on physical and mental health, time allocation, and disease causation (Kivimäki et al., 2015; ...). ... Second, the published studies lack a spatial perspective. The spatial environment plays a significant role in shaping behavior (Zhang et al., 2025). ..."

**真实例证 3**（Zhang 2019，"In light of the above review" + On one hand/On the other）：
> "In light of the above review, we argue that two research gaps in previous activity-space-based segregation studies can be identified and should be addressed. On one hand, few studies have considered the temporal variation in activity-space-based segregation. Most of the existing studies only examined activity-space-based segregation for one day. ..."

**真实例证 4**（Zheng 2026，引言版 First/Second/Third，综述版同构）：
> "However, there are still some limitations in the current research on overtime work: First, most studies focus on health impacts, institutional causes, or economic effects ... while few conduct geographical analysis of the activity patterns and spatial hotspots of urban overtime workers. Second, traditional research conducted by survey documents or interview data ... are constrained by sampling limitations and the respondents' memory bias. Third, existing research on overtime work mostly focuses on practitioners in specific industries, such as healthcare workers ..."

### 3.2 单线推进式（承认成就 → 指出被忽视的具体之处）

**真实例证 1**（AllenFarber 2020）：
> "However, despite this important theoretical discussion, there has been limited empirical research on how this can be implemented into existing transport planning practice."

**真实例证 2**（Toger 2023，对照式：枚举已有丰富度 vs 具体缺口）：
> "Although there is a wealth of general literature on equity and transportation (see e.g., McFadden, 1978; ...), a thorough evidence-based analysis of inequality in leisure travel is largely lacking in the existing literature. And therefore, a solid data-analytical examination seems to be pertinent to fill this gap in the research."

**真实例证 3**（DongMiller 2026，综述内"尚未有人做"）：
> "Yet few studies have systematically disentangled these within-group variations, particularly in comparing their commuting versus non-commuting days."

### 3.3 极简一句式（一句话直给空白，用于局部小节）

**真实例证 1**（2022_Zhang_7714）：
> "The impacts of social environments on multiple activity participation and duration with interactions constrained by budgets are not found in the literature."

**真实例证 2**（2026_Liu_7724）：
> "Currently, no single dataset offers both near-complete population coverage and detailed, semantically rich records of individual activities with high spatiotemporal resolution."

**真实例证 3**（Vitrano 2023，语境缺口，事实陈述即缺口）：
> "In Sweden, explicit time policies have never been implemented (Fernandes et al., 2015)."

### 3.4 Gap 后必接"本文做法/贡献"句（真实论文的固定动作）

- Xiao 2020："Yet, to date, there is a dearth of literature that examines the clustering patterns of people with different types of work schedule. **The research fills this gap in the literature on overtime work by visualizing the spatial and temporal distribution of people with different working hours in Shanghai** based upon big data analysis and spatial statistical methods."
- DongMiller 2026："This study **addresses these gaps** by explicitly modelling the interaction between work modality and actual commute status."
- Palm 2025（每节末段固定动作）："This study **extends** the research on shift worker wellbeing and TRSE by examining the effect of shift work on trip making for social purposes, including trip making with other family members."
- Zhang 2019："This paper **aims to help fill these two research gaps** by implementing an empirical study in Beijing."

## 四、引用与证据呈现方式（真实论文怎么做）

| 方式 | 说明 | 真实例证 |
|---|---|---|
| **证据群引用（example-led）** | 每条引用配一句研究发现，2-4 篇连举 | Zhang 2019 综述 P3（Schnell / Ellis / Burgess / Shareck 四条实证） |
| **引用簇（citation cluster）** | 主题句后括号连引 3-6 篇建立"共识"感 | Lee 2026 主题句一次引 5 篇；Toger 2023 "a wealth of general literature (McFadden, 1978; O'Kelly, 1994; ...)" |
| **报方法/报发现分层** | 综述里既要报"谁发现了什么"，也要给"用什么数据/方法" | 2017_Tan：分析方法是 geovisualization vs statistical measures 两派 |
| **点名具体化（naming）** | 关键文献点名 + 一句话具体贡献（"only one study ... Jones and Pebley (2014) employ both sample survey and census data ..."） | Wang & Li 2016："To the best of our knowledge, there is only one study in the literature that has made such an attempt: Jones and Pebley (2014) ..." |
| **少数字/少百分比** | 真实综述不堆"多少篇/百分之多少" | 全部所析论文：无一篇用"143 条句式/占比 X%"这种元统计 |

**引用格式**：APA 7th（作者, 年份），引用跟在整句句末不打断句子；定义句必须带出处。

## 五、综述与引言/方法的衔接（真实论文怎么接缝）

**综述 → 引言**：综述是引言的放大版（Zheng 2026 / Ta 2021 同构）；引言点到为止的 gap 在综述给出完整谱系。
**综述 → 方法**：综述最后一段的缺口必须被方法节逐项兑现（承诺-兑现闭环）。2020_Wang_8696 实测："缺口—问题—方法"三方镜像：引言列三个局限、综述铺概念谱系、方法逐条兑现。
**综述 → 研究问题/假设**：DongMiller 2026 在综述之后单设 Research questions 节，把缺口转成可检验假设（"三条可检验假设为骨架"）。Hu 2022 的假设先行（文字版 SEM 路径）同样承接综述的三条线索。

## 六、陷阱与红线（真实审稿/批注教训）

1. **❌ 主题句含糊**：每段第一句必须说清"这段要论证什么"。真实论文主题句三种功能明确（定义/报现状/转折推进），从不含糊。
2. **❌ 综述是流水账**：按年份/作者罗列，没有主线 → 不是论证链。真实论文每条线索都有推进（概念→谱系→现状→缺口）。
3. **❌ 把"两条线未交叉"当 gap**：真实论文的跨线综述（如 Wang & Li 2016 两股流）以"应互补/只有极少数尝试结合"表述，不用"两条文献线相互承认但交汇点未建立"这种句式（见 01-introduction.md 反模式）。
4. **❌ gap 停在空话**："More research is needed" / "研究不足"——要具体到"什么没做"（"尚未以[核心变量组合]分类人群"）。真实论文每条缺陷都落在"具体没做什么"。
5. **❌ 操作化定义缺失**：综述引到关键实证研究时必须给出"测什么/怎么测"（如 Kim 2024 用 time crunch index 测时间压力），不能只报结论。
6. **❌ 把本文方法写进综述**：综述只写"已有研究怎么做"，不写"本文沿用…操作化"（那是方法节的事）。
7. **❌ 引文不可核实**：引用的每一篇都必须能在 Zotero 库内核实（作者/年份/数据/发现），宁可少引不可错引。
8. **❌ 元统计堆砌**：不写"143 条句式/占比 X%"，真实论文没有这种写法。

## 八、句式语料库（真实提取，按用途取用，附出处）

**段首主题句（定义/总领）：**
- `[Concept] is a multi-dimensional phenomenon that involves the [dim1], [dim2], and [dim3] dimensions.`（Zhang 2019）
- `Time is limited on weekdays and longer commutes suggest that less time is therefore available for other activities.`（Hu 2022）

**段首主题句（报现状/报趋势）：**
- `As an important dimension of everyday life, [topic] has received increased attention during recent decades.`（Hu 2022）
- `In the past few years, an increasing number of studies have argued that [claim].`（Zhang 2019）
- `Research and urban policymakers are particularly concerned about the relationship between [A] and [B] because [reason].`（Gao 2022）

**证据呈现（谁/数据/发现）：**
- `In measuring [construct], [Author] used [data] to capture [measure]. They found that [finding].`（综合）
- `For instance, [Author] ([year]) emphasized that ...; [Author] ([year]) found ...; [Author] ([year]) found ...`（Zhang 2019）
- `To the best of our knowledge, there is only one study in the literature that has made such an attempt: [Author] ([year]) ...`（Wang & Li 2016）

**局限/缺口（单线推进）：**
- `However, despite this important [theoretical/empirical] discussion, there has been limited research on [specific missing work].`（AllenFarber 2020）
- `Yet few studies have systematically [done X], particularly in [specific dimension].`（DongMiller 2026）
- `Although there is a wealth of general literature on [A], a thorough [evidence-based] analysis of [B] is largely lacking in the existing literature.`（Toger 2023）

**综述末尾 gap（枚举缺陷）：**
- `Nevertheless, the understanding of [topic] is still at a nascent stage. First, ... Second, ...`（Ta 2021）
- `However, the studies reported in recent literature have two limitations. First, ... Second, ...`（Gao 2025）
- `In light of the above review, we argue that two research gaps ... can be identified and should be addressed. On one hand, ... On the other hand, ...`（Zhang 2019）

**Gap 后接本文做法：**
- `This paper aims to help fill these two research gaps by implementing an empirical study in [place].`（Zhang 2019）
- `The research fills this gap in the literature on [topic] by [approach].`（Xiao 2020）
- `This study extends the research on [topic] by examining [specific new dimension].`（Palm 2025）
