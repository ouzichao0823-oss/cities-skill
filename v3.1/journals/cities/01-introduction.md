## 〇、这一章的"工作"是什么

读者读完本章，要能回答四个问题：
1. **引言由哪几段组成、每段承担什么功能**——为什么城市研究引言的"标准形状"是漏斗（funnel），哪些环节可以调整、哪些不能缺；
2. **首句怎么写**——用现象/概念/数字/城市/矛盾/事件切入，拒绝万能模板（真实例句见 2.1）；
3. **Gap 段怎么写**——三种真实写法（单线推进 A→B→C / 枚举缺陷 / 极简一句），与文献综述章完全一致；gap 后必接本文做法句；
4. **贡献句与 roadmap 句怎么写**——真实论文的贡献动词与编号方式、结构预告的常见形态。

**判断标准**：把你的论文引言的每一段抽出来，都能回答"这一段在漏斗里干什么"；gap 句抄下来能直接对应到综述的 gap 段；Move 3 承诺的数据/方法/指标能在方法节逐项兑现（与 09-layering 接缝一一致）。

## 一、真实论文怎么组织：引言的漏斗与段落功能

### 1.1 引言各段功能表（数十篇提炼）

| 功能 | 典型段位 | 它回答的问题 | 真实例证（tag） |
|---|---|---|---|
| 现象锚定 | P1 | 为什么关心这个议题 | 2014_VanHolm_7957（政策现场）、Xiao2020（社会热点）、2026_Liu_7724（常识） |
| 研究现状/综述 | P2–P3 | 领域研究到什么程度 | 2016_Wang_8603（infant stage）、2023_Shi_7664（方法演进谱系）、2026_He_8940（视角并列） |
| 缺口 | P3–P5 | 具体没做什么 | 2024_Zhang_7449（three limitations）、Ta2021（nascent stage）、Vitrano2023（时间维被忽视） |
| 研究问题/目的 | P4–P5 | 本文要回答什么 | 2014_Wu_6549（We address two primary research questions）、2022_Hu_7955（编号 RQ） |
| 贡献声明 | P5–P6 | 本文补了什么 | 2022_Gao_7829（four-fold）、2025_Chen_7515（four main contributions） |
| 结构预告（roadmap） | 末段 | 论文怎么走 | 2024_Zhang_7449、Zheng2026、Kim2024 |

**为什么这样排（漏斗逻辑）**：城市研究引言几乎都是"从读者可感知的现象进入，逐层收窄到本文"。每一段都为下一段制造"为什么还需要"的问题——P1 立"现象重要"→ P2/P3 给"已有研究"→ gap 段说"已有研究漏了什么"→ 本文段说"我来补"→ roadmap 给"怎么走"。真实论文可以调换环节位置（案例独立成段、贡献提前、问题独立成节），但**功能不能缺**：缺 gap 的引言是综述，缺本文段的引言是文献评论。

### 1.2 段落数范围与常见变体

- **2 段极简版**：2014_VanHolm_7957（现象→理论→检验，贡献以"我检验了什么"呈现）、2026_Won_8833（P1 大背景+转折，P2 缺口+本文方案，"宽漏斗→窄缺口"）。
- **3 段紧凑版**：2016_Tammaru_8475（领域→aim+三点贡献→性别机制+RQ）、2024_Chen_5974（背景→理论+证据局限→问题+本研究）、AllenFarber2020（3 段+编号目标清单）。
- **4–7 段标准版（最多）**：典型 = P1 现象 → P2–P3 综述/缺口 → P4 本文 → P5 roadmap（2017_Tan_6973、2022_Gao_7829、2023_Tao_5710、2025_Chen_7515、2026_Ding_7711、Tao2020、Zheng2026 等）。
- **8–11 段长版（综述嵌入引言）**：2021_Cao_8109（8 段）、2023_Liu_8071（8 段）、2023_Qiao_5967（8 段）、2026_Chamberlain_8947（9 段）、2025_Azari_8061（11 段）——引言同时承担综述功能，缺口用 First/Second/Third 多段展开。
- **变体一：案例合理性独立成段**：2014_Wu_6549（"中国-南京"两段嵌在问题提出前）、2020_Wang_8696（P4 南京语境）、Xiao2020（P4 上海两理由）。
- **变体二：研究问题独立成段/小节**：2014_Wu_6549（P3 两句 RQ）、2026_Fan_8053（正文 4 段+问题列表段）、2026_Li_8817（1.1 Background + 1.2 研究问题 + 1.3 贡献）；也有论文把 RQ 放综述末（2021_Lu_5979、2026_Won_8833 放综述 2.4）。
- **变体三：贡献提前**：2016_Tammaru_8475 在 P2 就宣布 aim+三点贡献；2021_Cao_8109 放到 P7。
- **变体四：结果前置预告**：Kim2024（P4 "We find that..."）、2023_Tao_5710（P4 预览性结果）、AllenFarber2020（P3 发现预览）。
- **变体五：目标清单式**：AllenFarber2020 的 four targeted objectives——目标 = 研究问题 + 方法承诺 + 产出形式三位一体。

### 1.3 段落间过渡方式（漏斗怎么收）

真实论文的段间过渡是"钩子-回应"的链条，常用六类（均可在素材库找到）：
1. **让步-转折**：While / Although / However / Yet / Nevertheless / Despite——P1→P2、综述→缺口最常见（2017_Tan_6973、2023_Lu_8074、2026_Lee_7708）。
2. **显式回指**：To fill the abovementioned gaps / In response to the above deficiencies / To address these gaps / In light of the research gap——把前几段缺口打包成"本文要补什么"（2022_Gao_7829、2019_Zhang_7717、2026_Zhuang_8043）。
3. **钩子-回应**：P3 末尾"传统数据无能为力"→P4 开头"技术进展带来新数据"（2021_Cao_8109）；P2 说"主观数据难获取"→P3 说"我们找到一个稀缺数据集"（Kim2024）。
4. **词面钩子**：用上一段末句的词语开下一段——"Along with the bloom of demand side research, the supply side ... is unproportionally overlooked"（2023_Qiao_5967）；"The types of trips described so far are frequently labeled 'discretionary travel'"（Palm2025）。
5. **设问推进**："Why does compact development make people drive less? Most literature to date has tried to ..."（2021_Choi_7991）；2015_Zhou_7343 的三个连问句后立刻自答。
6. **时空/视角收窄**：global → China → 城市（2014_Wu_6549、2021_Poku-Boansi_7965）；"From a risk perspective ... From an accessibility perspective"（2026_He_8940）。

## 二、段落级写法（真实论文怎么做）

### 2.1 首句写法（现象锚定，多摘）

真实论文首句**没有一句是万能模板**，全部落到具体对象。六种真实切入：

**① 现象/政策现场切入**
- `Cities are constructing bike paths, financing public art, and encouraging diversity, all in an effort to attract knowledge workers.`（2014_VanHolm_7957）——骨架：`<Actors> are doing <三个并列动作>, all in an effort to <目标>.`；用读者能看见的政策现场立"为什么关心"。
- `E-scooter sharing, a two-wheel electric vehicle with a standing deck and a handling bar, has sprouted as a new micromobility in urban space.`（2025_Cao_3074）——骨架：`<Topic>, a <同位语定义>, has sprouted as <新类别>.`；先定义再定位。
- `Cities are complex and dynamic systems (Alexander, 1965; Batty, 2013).`（2026_Liu_7724）——骨架：`<对象> are complex and dynamic systems (<理论引用>).`；从第一性原理起句。

**② 概念定义切入**
- `Urban segregation can be understood as the uneven spatial distribution of different social groups, measured mainly by socioeconomic attributes such as income, occupation, gender, and ethnicity, over different municipal areas.`（2024_Zhang_7449）——骨架：`<核心概念> can be understood as <操作化定义> (<测度依据>).`；首句即定义全文关键词，后续共用这一定义。
- `Travel behavior refers to the spatial movement process that residents undertake to fulfill daily needs such as work, leisure, and living.`（2026_Fan_8053）——骨架：`<概念> refers to <定义>.`；定义句带出处。
- `Social exclusion, which is closely related to social and economic inequality, has been discussed for decades.`（2020_Wang_8696）——骨架：`<核心概念>, which is closely related to <更大议题>, has been discussed for decades (<引文>).`；"概念+地位"式。

**③ 数字/统计切入**
- `China's urbanisation level rose from 17.9% in 1978 to 49.68% in 2010 with a 3.24% annual growth rate.`（2015_Zhou_7343）——骨架：`<国家>'s <指标> rose from <数值> to <数值> with a <数值> annual growth rate (<来源>).`；用数据定调"快速变化"。
- `Night and evening shift workers (NESWs) make up 10–20% of the workforce in European, North American, and East Asian countries.`（Palm2025）——骨架：`<人群> make up <区间>% of the workforce in <区域列表> (<来源>).`；跨洲数字直接交代研究对象规模。

**④ 通俗常识切入**
- `A basic function of urban transportation is to enable participation in daily activities.`（AllenFarber2020）——骨架：`A basic function of <系统> is to enable <结果>.`；常识起句，为后文"规划忘了初衷"制造反差。
- `Tackling social inequality is one of the key goals of achieving the sustainable development of cities.`（2022_Gao_7829）——骨架：`Tackling <问题> is one of the key goals of achieving <更大目标>.`
- `Spatial segregation of economically vulnerable groups is a problem that has long been discussed in the geographical study of urban space.`（2021_Jang_7876）；`The spatial segregation of ethnic minorities and economically disadvantaged populations has been a defining attribute of urban America for nearly a century.`（2023_Lu_8074）——骨架：`<现象> has been a defining attribute of <地域> for nearly a century.`

**⑤ 具体城市/地区切入**
- `The problem of how to build employment opportunity and access for low-skill, urban residents is particularly an issue facing northern post-industrial cities.`（2018_Davidson_6227）——骨架：`The problem of how to build <资源> for <群体> is particularly an issue facing <城市类型>.`
- `In recent years, the number of new immigrants has been steadily increasing, especially in metropolitan areas.`（2026_Guo_8821）——骨架：`In recent years, the number of <人群> has been steadily increasing, especially in <地域>.`

**⑥ 学术史/争论/事件切入**
- `Urban spatial structure has long held an interest for those who study cities.`（2014_Wu_6549）——骨架：`<Topic> has long held an interest for those who study <领域>.`
- `There has been a long-standing discussion about the relationship between digital transformation and urban spatial structure, especially in terms of whether information and communications technology (ICT) leads to centralization or decentralization of urban functions.`（2026_Lee_7708）——骨架：`There has been a long-standing discussion about <议题>, especially in terms of whether <A> leads to <B or C>.`
- `The topic of "996" has recently sparked fierce debates in China.`（Xiao2020）；`In China, the phenomenon of overtime work represented by the "996" work system in the internet industry (working from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m., 6 days a week) has become a social topic of concern.`（Zheng2026）——骨架：`In <地点>, the phenomenon of <现象> represented by <具体例子> has become a social topic of concern (<引用>).`；社会热点切入，随后用统计锚点增强客观性。
- `Space is shaped by the individuals who engage with it daily (Lefebvre, 2012).`（2025_Chen_7515）——理论名言切入。
- `The aim of this paper is to shed more light on how workplace segregation is related to residential segregation and intermarriage among newly arrived immigrants.`（2016_Tammaru_8475）；`The goal of this research is to propose a data-driven and systematic framework ...`（2021_Ponce-Lopez_7967）——开宗明义式，较少见但存在。

**拒万能模板**：素材库 数十篇没有一篇用"With the rapid development of urbanization, ..."开篇。检验方法：把首句里的研究对象换成另一个学科，如果句子还成立，就不是城市研究引言的合格首句。

### 2.1b 开篇吸引力：直切研究空白，不做教科书式铺陈（爱思唯尔引言方法论，[DATE] 融入）

**核心判断**：引言决定审稿人和读者的第一印象。对熟悉领域的专业读者，先从定义、已知事实铺陈再进入问题（"教科书式"）会削弱研究问题；更好的做法是**尽早呈现尚未解决的问题或核心论点**。

**同一主题的两种开篇对比（爱思唯尔原文示例）**：
- ❌ 教科书式（先定义、先罗列已知事实）：`Multiple sclerosis (MS) is an immune-mediated disorder of the central nervous system that commonly affects young adults and can lead to long-term neurological disability. The disease is associated with inflammation, loss of myelin, and progressive damage to nerve cells, producing a broad spectrum of clinical symptoms.`
- ✅ 直切缺口式（第二句就落到"尚未解决什么"）：`Although multiple sclerosis (MS) is a well-studied neurological disorder, the biological processes driving primary progressive MS remain incompletely understood.`

**投稿前三问自查（爱思唯尔）**——往往只需改开头几句话，不必重写整段：
1. **是否有效引出研究空白？** 宽泛背景（"Cancer remains one of the leading causes of death worldwide"）对专业读者几乎没有新信息；改为具体缺口（"Although survival rates for several cancers have improved substantially, reliable biomarkers for predicting treatment resistance remain limited"）。
2. **是否聚焦在值得讨论的观点？** "XYZ is transforming healthcare" 是空背景；"Current XYZ diagnostic models can achieve high accuracy but consistently underperform when deployed across diverse populations" 是具体、有依据、值得讨论的论断。
3. **是否罗列过多背景信息？** 引言≠文献综述；新颖性和真正贡献不能被埋在大段泛泛背景之下。核心读者关注的是研究提出什么新问题、新观点、新发现。


### 2.2 Gap 段三写法（与 02-litreview 完全一致，整合旧版第三节精华）

**核心结论**：真实论文的 gap **几乎全是"单线推进型"**——承认主流研究做了什么，指出一个具体的被忽视之处（B），落到"本文做 C"。不存在"A 线总结 + B 线总结 + 两条线没合并"的双线结构（旧版"两线未交叉"反模式已于 [DATE] 删除，综述章 02-litreview 第三节同口径）。

**写法一：单线推进 A→B→C（主流）**
- `While these studies have grown knowledge on the characteristics of the reverse commuting population and their journey to work, they do not address to the same extent the demographic characteristics of the areas in which reverse commuters live in the city and in which they work in the suburbs.`（2018_Davidson_6227）——骨架：`While these studies have grown knowledge on <已知>, they do not address to the same extent <未知>.`
- `Although digital applications influence how they shop, relax, and interact socially, their spatial consequences, especially within the neighborhood, have not been explored deeply.`（2026_Lee_7708）——骨架：`Although <现象>, <其空间后果> have not been explored deeply (<文献>).`
- `Although a growing number of studies have examined the impact of the pandemic on people's travel behavior, little is known about how interactions with others changed during the pandemic as a result of changes in travel.`（2023_Lu_8074）——骨架：`Although <已有研究>, little is known about <新问题>.`
- `Less is known on how the two are related to each other.`（2016_Tammaru_8475）；`So far, there have been some attempts to reveal the ethnic difference in space-time behavior in Chinese cities based on descriptive analyses, while most studies ... tended to focus on other issues, such as poverty ... and gender inequalities.`（2017_Tan_6973）——骨架：`So far, there have been some attempts to <做某事>, while most studies on <议题> tended to focus on <其他议题>.`
- `Even if present and crucial in several definitions of access inequalities, the temporal dimension has not received extensive attention and spatial approaches have been instead favoured.`（Vitrano2023）——骨架：`Even if present and crucial in several definitions of <X>, the <维度> has not received extensive attention and <另一维度> have been instead favoured (<引文>).`（时间维度被忽视的 gap 句式）

**写法二：枚举缺陷（次主流，缺口较大时用）**
- `We identify three limitations in the existing urban segregation literature.` + First/Second/And finally（2024_Zhang_7449）——骨架：`We identify <N> limitations in the existing <领域> literature. First, ... Second, ... And finally, ...`（缺口与贡献同编号对位，见 2.3）
- `However, there are still some limitations in the current research on overtime work: First, most studies focus on health impacts ... Second, traditional research conducted by survey ... are constrained by sampling limitations and the respondents' memory bias. Third, existing research mostly focuses on practitioners in specific industries ...`（Zheng2026）——骨架：`However, there are still some limitations in the current research on <主题>: First, ... Second, ... Third, ...`
- `Nevertheless, the understanding of activity space-based segregation is still at a nascent stage. First, ... Second, ...`（Ta2021）；`The current understanding of the activity space of disadvantaged groups, particularly, the low-income group, is still incomplete. First, ... Second, ...`（Tao2020）——骨架：`The current understanding of <议题> is still incomplete. First, <缺口1>. Second, <缺口2>.`
- `However, the studies reported in recent literature have two limitations. First, in only a few studies have working hours been considered as a factor ... Second, the published studies lack a spatial perspective.`（Gao2025）——每条缺陷后附"为什么重要"+文献支撑。
- `However, available studies still present certain limitations. First, ... Second, ... Third, ...`（2023_Liu_8071）；`To summarize, existing literature exhibits three research gaps. First, ... Second, ... Last is ...`（2026_Li_8817）。

**写法三：极简一句式（局部缺口或小缺口）**
- `However, whether time scarcity due work and commute hours alters these associations is unknown.`（2023_Oostenbach_7984）——骨架：`However, whether <新因素> alters these associations is unknown.`
- `This also holds for leisure mobility, although leisure related activity spaces are understudied.`（Toger2023）——骨架：`... although <子话题> are understudied.`
- `To date, this body of scholarship has not addressed feelings of time pressure.`（Kim2024）；`Yet, the effect of these dynamics on household travel behavior remains largely unexplored, despite a significant portion of the workforce working at these times.`（Palm2025）——骨架：`Yet, the effect of <这些机制> on <结果> remains largely unexplored, despite <规模事实>.`
- `Despite this shift, there remains a lack of conceptual clarity regarding what is being segregated, at what spatial scale, and through which mechanisms.`（2026_Li_8809）——骨架：`Despite this shift, there remains a lack of <概念清晰度> regarding <并列三问>.`（精确 gap 的范本：把"不清楚"落到三个具体问题）

**gap 后必接本文做法句（真实论文的固定动作）**：
- `To fill the abovementioned gaps, this study proposed a framework for quantifying transport inequality ... based on two kinds of human mobility data.`（2022_Gao_7829）——骨架：`To fill the abovementioned gaps, this study <数据/方法/城市>.`
- `In response to the above deficiencies of past research in both conceptual and practical senses, this paper draws upon notions of the new mobilities paradigm and theoretical constructs of time geography ... to examine ... in Beijing, China.`（2019_Zhang_7717）
- `This study aims to fill these research gaps by using mobile phone data to measure urban segregation of migrants in Longgang, China.`（2024_Zhang_7449）；`This study addresses the gap by using large-scale smartphone mobility data to examine how major roads influence park visitation patterns.`（2026_Won_8833）；`This research seeks to bridge the above knowledge gaps by measuring and comparing accessibility to some main non-work facilities ...`（2023_Tao_5710）；`To fill this gap, this paper builds a connection between street environment, travel experience, and e-scooter mode choice.`（2025_Cao_3074）；`To address these gaps, this paper draws on the research framework of social sensing and uses daily commuting characteristics extracted from mobile phone data to identify typical BCs.`（2026_Zhuang_8043）。

**反模式（真实语料中不存在）**：①"两条文献线相互承认但交汇点未建立"；②"X 研究未触及 Y 本身"且 Y 就是 X 的研究对象（gap 必须落在"具体没做什么"）；③"More research is needed"式空话。

### 2.3 贡献声明句（真实例句：方法/数据/视角/尺度类）

**编号式贡献（最常用，动词 = contributes to / makes / offers / advances）**：
- `It contributes to the segregation literature in three important ways. First, it brings forward the ongoing discussions on segregation as a multidimensional process ... Second, we use longitudinal data, while most existing studies on segregation use cross-sectional data. Third, we study workplace segregation of newly arrived immigrants separately for men and women.`（2016_Tammaru_8475）——骨架：`It contributes to the <领域> literature in <N> important ways. First, ... Second, ... Third, ...`
- `The main contributions of this study are four-fold: 1) This study documents transport inequality using people-based activity spaces instead of place-based accessibility ... 2) In addition to ... inter-group activity disparity at the zone level is measured ... 4) This study explores the factors responsible for the activity disparities between the two groups and the spatial heterogeneity.`（2022_Gao_7829）——骨架：`The main contributions of this study are four-fold: 1) ... 2) ... 3) ... 4) ...`
- `This research contributes to the existing literature by (1) utilizing a large sample of fine-grained mobile phone dataset ... (2) performing a trend-driven analysis by conducting K-means time series clustering ... (3) conducting statistical analysis ... (4) discussing possible policy implications derived from our findings.`（2023_Lu_8074）——骨架：`This research contributes to the existing literature by (1) <数据贡献>; (2) <方法贡献>; (3) <分析贡献>; (4) <政策贡献>.`（手机信令 + K-means）
- `In summary, this paper makes four main contributions: First, it investigates ... from a spatiotemporal perspective. ... Moreover, Examining Zhuhai as a case study can deepen the understanding of gender segregation in Chinese cities ...`（2025_Chen_7515）——骨架：`In summary, this paper makes <N> main contributions: First, ... Second, ... Moreover, <案例贡献>.`
- `The contributions of this paper are twofold. Theoretically, it offers a renewed understanding of ... Methodologically, it proposes a transferable and scalable framework ...`（2026_Zhuang_8043）——骨架：`The contributions of this paper are twofold. Theoretically, ... Methodologically, ...`（理论/方法二分式）

**句子式贡献（不编号，靠动词立贡献）**：
- 视角类：`In this light, this research attempts to contribute to the literature by exploring the causal relationships between ethnicity and people's space-time behavior through controlling for other factors such as income, residential environment, or social networks.`（2017_Tan_6973）——骨架：`In this light, this research attempts to contribute to the literature by exploring <因果问题> through controlling for <混杂因素>.`
- 数据类：`This study aims to offer new insights into age disparities in urban commuting patterns using large-scale mobile signaling data and community census survey data, with Beijing as a case study.`（2024_Chen_5974）——骨架：`This study aims to offer new insights into <议题> using <数据>, with <城市> as a case study.`（手机信令数据贡献的模板句）
- 尺度类：`By linking individual behavioural heterogeneity with structural functional disparities, this study advances the understanding of transport inequality from the urban to the regional scale and provides new empirical evidence for promoting equitable regional integration.`（2026_Liu_8807）——骨架：`By linking <微观因素> with <结构因素>, this study advances the understanding of <议题> from <尺度1> to <尺度2> and provides new empirical evidence for <政策目标>.`
- 框架类：`Compared with existing studies, this study offers an explanatory framework that effectively integrates spatial and social dimensions in understanding the mechanisms of residential differentiation.`（2026_Ding_7711）——骨架：`Compared with existing studies, this study offers <框架> that effectively integrates <维度> in understanding <机制>.`
- 增强类：`This study enriches the existing body of literature by verifying the composite effects of various BE factors while clarifying the independent effects of street configuration, eventually providing improved planning strategies ...`（2022_Li_7394）；`This study breaks new ground by examining how night and evening shifts affect two aspects of travel behavior.`（Palm2025）；`We argue that comparing segregation via people's actual and potential activity space and examining the temporal dimension of segregation will greatly enrich our theoretical understanding of social segregation and provide valuable knowledge for developing effective social integration policies.`（2019_Zhang_7717）。

**克制定位（防止贡献夸大）**：真实论文用限定词给贡献"设防"——`To the best of our knowledge, few research has addressed the mediating role of travel experience ...`（2025_Cao_3074）；`The present study was devised as a preliminary test of the potential of mobile phone data, with Nanjing ... The novelty of this study lies in clarifying the influence of working patterns on urban residents' outdoor leisure activities on weekends, revealing spatial variations ... and providing new perspectives ...`（Gao2025）——骨架：`The novelty of this study lies in <动词-ing 1>, <动词-ing 2>, and <动词-ing 3>.`；避免空泛"首次/首创"。

### 2.4 Roadmap 句（"The remainder of this paper..."类）

**标准逐节版（最常用）**：
- `The rest of the paper unfolds as follows: Section 2 reviews the relevant literature, followed by the introduction of study area and data in Section 3. Section 4 proposes the methods used to identify migrants' working and residential places and measure their segregation patterns. Section 5 presents the main results from our analysis. We end our paper with a conclusion and discussion in Section 6.`（2024_Zhang_7449）——骨架：`The rest of the paper unfolds as follows: Section <N> <动词> <内容>. ... We end our paper with <结论节>.`
- `The structure of the rest of this article is as follows: Section 2 is a literature review. Section 3 describes the data and methods. Section 4 presents the results and findings of the research. Section 5 is a discussion of the research results. Section 6 summarizes the article and discusses future research directions.`（Zheng2026）
- `The rest of the study is structured as follows. Section 2 provides a brief review of relevant literature. Section 3 introduces the study context, data sources and methods employed. Section 4 presents the results from a series of spatial and statistical analysis. Main findings are summarized and recommendations are proposed in Section 5 before conclusions are drawn.`（2023_Tao_5710）
- `The remainder of this paper includes the following sections. Section 2 reviews related research. Section 3 introduces the methods. Data and model specifications are presented in Section 4. Policy implications are discussed in Section 5. Section 6 concludes this study.`（2022_Zhang_7714）

**内容动词版（逐句预告"做什么"而不只报章节名）**：
- `In the following, we further elaborate the importance and uniqueness of hukou-based divisions in Chinese cities. Then, we review the literature on accessibility. Next, we introduce the study area, data and methodology. The empirical analysis that follows depicts the basic characteristics of job accessibility for different hukou holders. Based on these results we conduct scenario simulations ... The final section concludes.`（2017__6166）——骨架：`In the following, we further elaborate <A>. Then, we review <B>. Next, we introduce <C>. The empirical analysis that follows depicts <D>. Based on these results we conduct <E>. The final section concludes.`
- `We begin by discussing the scholarship on the 'spatial mismatch' hypothesis, then turn to a discussion of the transportation planning literature on reverse commuting.`（2018_Davidson_6227）——骨架：`We begin by <综述1>, then turn to <综述2>.`

**极简版与内容导航版**：
- `The remainder of this article is organized as follows.`（2021_Cao_8109）；`This paper is structured as follows.`（2016_Wang_8603）；`The rest of the paper is organized as follows.`（2014_Wu_6549）——骨架：`The remainder of this paper is organized as follows.`（后接逐节句或直接进入 Section 2）
- `The following section describes the relevant research on time poverty. We then summarize our methodological approach and data. Next, we describe our findings, and then we discuss the implications for research and policy.`（Kim2024）——骨架：`The following section describes <综述>. We then summarize <方法>. Next, we describe <结果>, and then we discuss <含义>.`
- `The following section delves into the existing literature on these topics.`（Palm2025）；`The structure of this paper is organized as follows: following the introduction section, a literature review of related research is presented in Section 2.`（2023_Shi_7664）。

### 2.5 中文作者 Cities 引言特点（中国案例/信令数据/政策背景）

**① 中国案例段/段组（单城市必须论证案例价值）**：
- `More specifically Nanjing, the capital of Jiangsu Province, provides some contrast with the more commonly studied and much larger trio of Chinese super cities, Shanghai, Beijing and Guangzhou.`（2014_Wu_6549）——骨架：`More specifically <案例> provides some contrast with the more commonly studied <基准案例>.`；用"与超大城市对比"论证案例选择价值。
- `Over the past four decades, China has undergone the most extensive and rapid urbanization process in world history, with more than 700 million people migrating from their rural hometowns.`（2026_Ding_7711）——骨架：`Over the past <时间>, <国家> has undergone <程度词> urbanization, with <数字> people migrating from <来源>.`
- `In the Chinese context, the spatial distribution of ethnic minorities at different scales in China are quite different from those found in Western countries.`（2017_Tan_6973）——骨架：`In the <语境> context, <语境特征> are quite different from those found in <西方>.`
- `Shanghai is chosen as the empirical case analysis for two reasons. First, ... Second, ...`（Xiao2020）；`In the empirical analysis, Guangzhou—a first-tier Chinese megacity undergoing rapid urbanization and polycentric restructuring—is selected as the case study.`（2026_Zhuang_8043）——骨架：`<城市> is chosen as the empirical case analysis for <N> reasons. First, <理由1>. Second, <理由2>.` / `In the empirical analysis, <城市>—<破折号内嵌选城理由>—is selected as the case study.`
- 非中国范本同法：`Tokyo offers an interesting case because its spatial segregation has not received adequate scrutiny compared to well-studied Western cities, and its residents exhibit a high level of income diversity.`（2024_Sun_7832）；`Hong Kong provides an interesting study context, given the prevalence of the poverty issue ...`（Tao2020）——骨架：`<案例> offers an interesting case because <理由1>, and <理由2>.`

**② 手机信令/大数据数据机会段（中国城市研究高频）**：
- `With the rapid development of information and communication technologies, urban research increasingly benefits from diverse sources of high-density spatio-temporal data, such as social media records, taxi GPS trajectories, public transportation smart card data, and mobile signaling data.`（Zheng2026）——骨架：`With the rapid development of <技术>, urban research increasingly benefits from diverse sources of <数据类>, such as <数据1>, <数据2>, ... and <数据X>.`
- `Among these, mobile signaling data is particularly advantageous due to its extensive population coverage, high temporal frequency, and relatively precise spatial resolution.`（Zheng2026）——骨架：`Among these, <数据X> is particularly advantageous due to its <优势1>, <优势2>, and <优势3>.`
- `In recent years, the rapid advancement of mobile positioning technologies has offered abundant data resources for travel behavior research.`（2026_Fan_8053）——骨架：`In recent years, <技术进步> has offered abundant data resources for <研究领域>.`

**③ 政策/制度背景段**：
- `With significant implications for urban planning practice, China's market-oriented reform and people's increasing mobility have changed their daily life tremendously, which also complicated the social segregation in Chinese cities.`（2019_Zhang_7717）——骨架：`With significant implications for <政策领域>, <背景事件> have changed <对象> tremendously, which also complicated <主现象>.`
- `These, in addition to the People's Republic's rather unique institutional setting such as the persistence of the hukou divide, should be taken into consideration in analysing jobs/housing relations and measuring accessibility in Chinese cities.`（2017__6166）——把制度变量作为"必须考虑"的语境。

## 三、陷阱与红线（从素材提炼的审稿教训）

1. **❌ 首句万能模板**：数十篇无一篇用"With the rapid development of urbanization, ..."开篇。检验：首句换研究对象后是否仍成立？成立 = 不是城市研究引言的合格首句。
2. **❌ gap 空泛**："More research is needed" / "研究不足" / "很少人研究"——必须指明"具体没做什么"。范本是 2026_Li_8809 的三连问式 gap（"what is being segregated, at what spatial scale, and through which mechanisms"）。
2b. **❌ 教科书式开头**：先从定义、已知事实铺陈再进入问题（爱思唯尔反例：MS 定义三连句）。检验：删掉开头两三句后，研究空白是否仍然可读？可读 = 开头是噪音。正确做法见 2.1b（直切缺口式）。
2c. **❌ gap 落在"别人研究重点不在 X"**：如"X 并不是他们的研究重点"——这是价值判断（评判他人选题），既显傲慢、又招"凭什么要求别人研究这个"的质疑。正确落法=客观描述研究现状本身："X 与 Y 之间的联系还很少有研究加以考察"（Few studies have examined the link between ...）。（gap 要落在"该联系本身研究尚少"，不是"别人不研究这个"。）
3. **❌ "两条文献线相互承认但交汇点未建立"**：真实论文即使研究本身就是跨两条线（如时间使用 × 活动空间），也只以"忽视 B"单线表述——本反模式已于 [DATE] 核查删除，与 02-litreview 同口径。
4. **❌ 贡献夸大**：避免空泛"首次/首创"；真实论文用 `To the best of our knowledge, few research has addressed ...`（2025_Cao_3074）与 `The present study was devised as a preliminary test ...`（Gao2025）克制定位。
5. **❌ 无 roadmap**：多数真实论文末段有结构预告；没有 roadmap 的极简版（2014_VanHolm、2026_Won）也用贡献/主张句收尾，不会"突然结束"。
6. **❌ 研究问题悬空**：引言列的 RQ 必须被方法/结果逐条回答（承诺-兑现）。2024_Zhang_7449 的三缺口—三贡献—三 highlights 编号对位是最省力闭环（见 09-layering 3.4）。
7. **❌ 引言承诺的数据/结果没报**：Move 3 说了"数据 + 多指标 + 分组"，结果节漏一个指标 = 断链（09-layering 接缝一）。
8. **❌ 案例无理由**：单城市研究必须论证案例合理性（2014_Wu 南京对比、Xiao2020 上海两理由、Tao2020 香港）。
9. **❌ 关键概念首次出现无定义**：2022_Zhang_7714 用 "are referred to as" 锁定核心术语；2024_Zhang_7449 首句即给操作化定义。
10. **❌ 元统计堆砌**：不写"数十篇里 X 篇用 4 段"（本指南仅供写作参考，论文正文绝不出现元统计）。
11. **❌ 时间维度只当背景一笔带过**：若时间维度是解释视角，引言必须显式把它立为"被忽视的维度"，并在综述逐篇给操作化定义。

## 四、自查清单（机械可执行）

□ 首句属于六种真实切入之一（现象/概念/数字/常识/城市/争论或事件/目标声明），且不是万能模板？
□ 开篇直切研究空白（爱思唯尔问 1）：第一二句是否落在"尚未解决/尚不明确/存在争议"的问题上，而非教科书式铺陈定义与已知事实（见 2.1b）？
□ 聚焦值得讨论的具体观点（爱思唯尔问 2）：是"Current models achieve high accuracy but underperform across diverse populations"式论断，而非"XX is transforming XX"式空背景？
□ 背景信息不过量（爱思唯尔问 3）：引言不是文献综述；删掉背景段后研究价值仍清晰可见？
□ 引言整体是漏斗（现象锚定→研究现状→缺口→本文做法→roadmap），每段能说出"它在漏斗里承担什么功能"？
□ 有 gap 段/句，且是三种真实写法之一（单线推进 A→B→C / 枚举缺陷 / 极简一句），落在"具体没做什么"？
□ gap 后紧跟本文做法句（To fill / To address / To bridge / In response to ...）？
□ 没有"两条文献线相互承认但未交叉"反模式？没有"More research is needed"空话？
□ 贡献句动词真实（contributes to / advances / offers / enriches / breaks new ground / fills the gap），且落在方法/数据/视角/尺度具体一类？
□ 贡献声明克制（不用空泛"首次"；参考 "to the best of our knowledge" / "a preliminary test"）？
□ 研究问题（如有）能在方法/结果找到逐条回答（编号对位）？
□ 有 roadmap 段（The remainder of this paper is organized as follows...）或等效收尾（贡献/主张句）？
□ 单城市案例有选择理由（对比 / 两理由 / 情境合理性）？
□ 关键概念首次出现有定义（are referred to as / can be understood as）？
□ 全部英文例句逐字与素材库一致、可回库核实？
