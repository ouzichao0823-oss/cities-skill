# Cities 结论节写作指南（真实语料版）

> **数据基础**：Zotero 库内真实 Cities 论文"结论 Conclusion"节精提取 数十篇（cities_section_lib/sec_H.md，对应 cities_ds_parse/ 逐篇精提取）。全部例句逐字摘录自原文，可回库核实。
> **[DATE] 重写原则（改写说明，推翻 v1 的模板化写法）**：
> 1. 写作逻辑只能从 Zotero 库真实 Cities 论文提炼；**你自己的论文是被指导对象，不是语料来源**。
> 2. 不是罗列"多少篇/百分之多少/多少条句式"，而是提炼"结论为什么按这个顺序写""哪些信息必须有、哪些可以砍"等真正有用的写作判断，配中英例子，让英文不好的模型照骨架写出合格英文。
> **v1 遗留问题（已修正）**：①v1 把"压缩重述→城市含义→规划落点→弱势群体→个案一般化"写成城市研究万能范式，实测真实结论的组织远不止一种（合并节/无独立节/政策压轴/单段压缩/两段式都存在）；②v1 句式语料部分无出处或来自你的稿件，现在每条都有真实论文出处；③v1 缺少"结论与摘要/引言如何呼应"的指导——这正是结论最容易写成摘要复读的根源。

## 〇、这一章的"工作"是什么

读者（编辑、审稿人、导师）读完你的结论，要能回答四个问题：
1. **研究问题被回答了吗**（结论必须正面收束 RQ——不是重述方法，而是给出"答案是 X"的完整判断）；
2. **贡献是什么**（填补了什么缺口/提供了什么新视角——贡献声明在结论正式出场）；
3. **该做什么**（政策/实践落点，落到具体行动者与受益群体）；
4. **还缺什么**（局限与未来，诚实划定边界并为后来者指路）。

**判断标准**：结论不是摘要的重复，也不是讨论的摘要，而是论证链的**收束段**——把结果"翻译"成对研究问题、对学科、对城市实践的意义。如果删掉结论读者仍能靠摘要+讨论拼出全部信息，说明结论没有新增价值；反过来，如果结论出现摘要/结果里没有的新发现、新引用，也是错误。

## 一、真实论文怎么组织（数十篇实测）

### 1.1 主序：recap→发现→贡献→政策→局限→未来（最常见，但顺序可变）

多数结论按"总结发现（recap）→ 贡献 → 政策 → 局限 → 未来"推进（2018_Davidson、2022_Hu、2023_Lu、2024_Sun、2025_Azari；Zheng2026 两段式）。**顺序本身不是模板，顺序的背后是功能**——每段回答读者一个问题：

| 顺序 | 功能 | 真实例证 |
|---|---|---|
| **recap 总结发现** | 压缩复述"做了什么+发现什么"，与摘要/结果同序；可复述关键数字，但不逐条重报 | "This study has examined the activity-space-based segregation of the residents of different types of housing in Beijing in terms of both actual and potential activity space."（2019_Zhang_7717） |
| **贡献声明** | 填补什么缺口/提供什么新视角；与引言 Move 2 的 gap 精确对接 | "This paper adds to the robust body of equity-oriented, transportation planning research through building and discussing commute pattern typologies built on socio-demographic characteristics at both home and workplace."（2018_Davidson_6227） |
| **政策落点** | 把发现翻译成行动清单，落到行动者与受益群体 | "social policies should support the equitable distribution of urban resources, especially employment opportunities and social facilities ... for low-income individuals relocated to the suburbs."（2020_Wang_8696） |
| **局限** | 诚实声明边界（数据/方法/外推） | "We acknowledge that this study has limitations that warrant future research."（2024_Chen_5974） |
| **未来** | 每条局限对应的下一步，或开放性议程 | "For future research, it would be worthwhile to conduct activity-travel diary surveys over multiple days..."（Tao2020） |

**为什么按这个顺序写**：与读者决策顺序同构——先确认"研究做完了、有答案"（recap+RQ 收束），再确认"值得发表"（贡献），再确认"有用"（政策），最后"可信"（局限的诚实性）。2018_Davidson 是标准范本：发现→贡献→政策→局限→未来，且政策篇幅最大、是全文落脚点。

### 1.2 与 Discussion 合并：非常常见，结论承担什么

数十篇里相当一部分结论与讨论合并：2015_Zhou 标题即 "Conclusion and discussion"；2019_Zhang "6. Conclusion and discussion"；2021_Lu、2021_Cao、2023_Qiao "5. Conclusions and discussion"、2023_Tao、2024_Chen、2024_Zhang "Discussion and conclusion"、Ta2021、Palm2025 "Conclusions and implications"、2026_Zhuang、2026_Li_8809。**合并节内结论承担三件事：压缩总结发现 + 贡献声明 + 政策落点**；机制解释、与文献对话、理论展开归讨论；局限有的放结论（2020_Wang），有的留在讨论（2026_He "局限已在 4.3 处理，结论不重复"、2026_Wang_8274、2023_Oostenbach、2026_Fan）。
- 2026_Zhuang 是最清晰模板：6.1 Major findings（总结+贡献）/ 6.2 Policy implications（政策）/ 6.3 Limitations（局限+未来）。
- 2026_Fan 示范合并时的"压缩收束"：首句复述框架，中段编号复述三大发现，末段双重贡献收束——不再展开机制。
- **判断标准**：合并时若结论功能只剩一句"we discussed..."就是失职；反过来把讨论内容搬进结论会拖沓。

### 1.3 功能顺序变体（真实存在，不是错误）

- **政策压轴**：Xiao2020（发现→局限→贡献/未来→政策，作者注明是 Land Use Policy 期刊惯例）。
- **无独立局限段**：2017__6166（无显式局限段，贡献隐含在"首次用可达性模型×hukou 分组研究广州"的表述中）；2021_Jang（可推广性作为第三条贡献正面对接未来）；2023_Oostenbach（局限已在讨论处理）。
- **单段压缩式**：2026_Won 一段走完"发现→概念标签→机制→实践启示→公平愿景"，不复述数字，只做概念升华。
- **两段式**：Zheng2026（第一段总结+贡献+政策，第二段局限+未来）。
- **无独立结论节，由小节/段落承担**：2016_Tammaru（1.4 末两段）、2016_Wang（Section 6 后半）、2026_Ding（5.1–5.3 分步）、Tao2020（Section 5）——这些论文的"结论功能"依然完整，只是不叫 Conclusion。

### 1.4 结论与摘要/引言的呼应（真实怎么闭环）

- **与摘要同序回收**：2023_Shi 按摘要-结果顺序回收；2024_Sun "发现条目与摘要结果完全同序，形成闭环"；2026_Liu_8807 全部编号列举、与摘要和结果一一呼应；2025_Chen 摘要的"中心-外围"与结论 "central urban core being female-dominated and the peripheral industrial zones male-dominated" 同义复现。
- **核心结论逐字呼应**：2017_Tan 机制性核心结论与摘要结论句逐字呼应；2021_Lu 开头复述三 key outcomes 与摘要逐字呼应；Zheng2026 政策价值句与摘要意义句高度呼应。
- **首尾闭环**：2022_Gao 首尾分别对应 Abstract 的缺口与意义句；2023_Liu 首尾呼应摘要背景句（"Urban structure is a crucial and complex concept that greatly influences the livability and sustainability of cities."）。
- **回答引言 Move 2 的 Gap**：2017_Tan "this research seeks to fill an important knowledge gap through investigating whether ethnicity exerts an independent and significant influence on people's space-time behavior"；2017__6166 结论 P1 重述缺口（可达性研究在中国稀少+忽略 hukou）；2024_Sun 重述缺口（"relatively few studies have performed a detailed comparison..."）；2026_Chamberlain 复述研究问题（How/where/what patterns）。
- **写作含义**：结论不是从零开始的文本，而是与摘要、引言 gap 形成"同一论证链"的闭环；起草结论前先取出摘要的结果句与引言的 gap 句，逐条核对是否被收束。

## 二、功能块写法（结论的内部组织）

| 功能块 | 功能 | 常见内容 | 真实例证 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RQ 收束** | 正面回答研究问题 | We conclude / To conclude / Altogether | 2016_Tammaru、2017_Tan、Ta2021、2026_Zhuang |
| **贡献声明** | 填补缺口/新视角/新框架 | contributes to / offers / adds to / provides | 2019_Zhang、2026_Fan、Vitrano2023、2026_Liu_7724 |
| **政策落点** | 行动清单+行动者+受益群体 | should / recommend / provide support / It is necessary | 2020_Wang、Zheng2026、2015_Zhou、2026_Wang_8274 |
| **局限** | 诚实边界 | We acknowledge / some limitations | 2024_Chen、2024_Sun、2023_Liu |
| **未来** | 下一步议程 | future research / it would be worthwhile / we hope | 2017_Tan、Tao2020、Toger2023 |
| **收尾句** | 论断/升华/号召/愿景 | It is time / This is not just / Ultimately | 2017__6166、Palm2025、AllenFarber2020 |

### 2.1 一句话回答研究问题（RQ 收束句）

结论的"第一使命"是正面给出"研究问题的答案"，常用 We conclude / To conclude / Altogether 起句，把机制或判断一次性说清：
- "To conclude, changes in residential segregation and intermarriages with natives are differently related to workplace segregation for immigrant men and women arriving in Sweden from the Global South."（2016_Tammaru_8475）
- "We conclude that two major ethnic characteristics of the Hui minorities, i.e. gender division of domestic labor and the participation in spatially and temporally fixed daily religious activities, have led to an independent and significant influence of ethnicity on space-time behavior when compared with the Han majorities in Xining."（2017_Tan_6973）
- "By combining these two measurements, this paper indicates that activity space-based segregation exists in both the spatial and social dimensions."（Ta2021）
- "Altogether, these findings demonstrate that BCs are not a homogeneous category but instead encompass distinct subtypes shaped by distinct spatial configurations, accessibility conditions, and development pathways."（2026_Zhuang_8043）

**写作判断**：RQ 收束句要含"答案的关键词"（变量、机制、群体），让读者不看摘要也能独立判断"研究回答了什么"；避免 "the results are interesting / worth discussing" 这类无信息收束。你的论文的答案形态是"你的分组+核心变量独立维度"，收束句应把"分组"与"独立维度"两个关键词同时放进一句。

### 2.2 贡献声明句（你的重点：视角/维度创新类）

真实贡献句都有"具体做法/具体对象"支撑，动词克制。按功能分四类：

- **填补缺口/扩展文献**（by/through + 具体做法）：2018_Davidson "This paper adds to the robust body of <field> through <what was built>."；2020_Wang "This research seeks to contribute to the existing literature on accessibility and social exclusion by portraying a more realistic picture of accessibility, which can provide a useful reference for related research."；2014_Wu "This is a new contribution of this study to the understanding of <topic>, compared with those using <alternative data/scales>."
- **新视角/新维度**（你"核心变量新视角"直接对位）：
  - "These findings suggest that space-time behavior provides a helpful perspective for achieving a deeper understanding of socio-spatial segregation through focusing on the dynamic nature of everyday life."（2017_Tan_6973）骨架：`<X> provides a helpful perspective for achieving a deeper understanding of <对象> through focusing on <新焦点>.`
  - "In this paper, we focused on the temporal dimension as a crucial facet of unequal time geographies of accessibility, showing how its understanding is an increasingly important prerequisite for the planning of an accessible, equitable and attractive PT."（Vitrano2023）骨架：`In this paper, we focused on <新维度> as a crucial facet of <领域>, showing how its understanding is an increasingly important prerequisite for the planning of <目标>.`
  - "This study advances our understanding of <主题> by quantifying a <概念标签> between <理论状态> and <实际行为>."（2026_Won_8833）
- **新框架**：2026_Fan "Overall, this study offers a new analytical framework for understanding the relationship between urban travel behavior and the built environment."；2023_Liu "offering a new research perspective and empirical foundation for comprehensively depicting and measuring urban structure."
- **理论+应用分层**：2026_Liu_7724 "Based on these findings, our study makes contributions at methodological, theoretical, and applied levels. Methodologically, ... Theoretically, ... Practically, ..."
- **首创声明（慎用）**：Kim2024 "This is the first paper that comprehensively examines the time crunch index available in the Canadian GSS."；2023_Oostenbach "Our study was the first to explore whether the urban concept of 20MN moderates..."——first 只出现在真实首创的论文且都有具体对象；你的论文不要用。

**防止 overclaim 的配套句**：Kim2024 "Notwithstanding these results, it is important to stress that time crunch and time poverty are just one dimension of transportation poverty and inequity."——单维度贡献论文应在贡献后立刻声明边界，既诚实又防审稿人挑刺。

### 2.3 政策/实践含义句（与讨论政策段的区别）

**结论里的政策 = 落点清单**（把发现翻译成"该做什么"，给行动者+受益群体，动词直接）；**讨论里的政策段 = 论证**（机制解释、与文献对话、多方案权衡）。真实结论政策句有四种写法：
- **建议+行动者+受益群体**：2020_Wang "These findings suggest that to improve the health and quality of life of the low-income group and to create adequate, convenient and appropriate activity opportunities for the vulnerable groups, social policies should support the equitable distribution of urban resources, especially employment opportunities and social facilities (...) for low-income individuals relocated to the suburbs."——政策对象=结果里的最脆弱群体。
- **实证支撑政策（时间/工作类，你的主题高度相关）**：Zheng2026 "These insights provide empirical evidence to support industrial policy formulation, labor protection strategies, and urban planning aimed at improving work-life balance and promoting sustainable urban development."；Palm2025 "Given the significant share of the population working these hours in many countries (...), it is time for transportation research and planning to better understand these workers' behaviours and their households' needs."
- **研究导向政策（证据不足时的落点）**：2017_Tan "With respect to policy implications, it is important to undertake further research to explore whether <群体> face greater disadvantage in mobility and accessibility and are thus more likely to experience social exclusion."
- **策略序列/主张升级**：2026_Wang_8274 "Under resource constraints, a more effective strategy is to first raise street connectivity to a basic acceptable level and, on this basis, prioritize improvements in the quantity, spatial structure, and service quality of nearby facilities."；2026_Lee "Rather than adopting the 15-minute city as a universal blueprint, policy should focus on calibrating the desirable level of compactness in land use and infrastructure in accordance with the existing urban morphology."；2018_Davidson "Policy around <problem> should not only focus on <A>, but also on <B>."

**与摘要政策句的关系**：摘要的政策句是结论政策句的一句话版；结论里展开行动者与受益群体。

### 2.4 局限与未来研究句

**局限起句模板（真实 6 种）**：
- "However, this study has some limitations and future studies are needed to address them."（2019_Zhang_7717）
- "Inevitably, this study has some limitations."（2023_Liu_8071）
- "We acknowledge that this study has limitations that warrant future research."（2024_Chen_5974）
- "Some limitations of this study should be noted."（2022_Li_7394）
- "There are several limitations and opportunities for future studies."（2022_Gao_7829）
- "Several limitations point to clear next steps."（2026_Li_8809）

**真实写法要点**：
- **编号逐条**：Firstly/Secondly/In addition（2024_Chen）、First/Second/Third（2023_Qiao）、On the one hand/On the other hand + Therefore（2020_Wang）。
- **每条局限带未来方案（最佳实践）**：2023_Liu 三条局限每条例"未来该怎么做"；2026_Lee 每条=局限→原因→未来方案；2020_Wang "Therefore, future research should try to use more accurate methods and measures based on GPS data, such as network-based GPS tracking buffers."
- **外推性警告单独成句**：2018_Davidson "It is important to note, however, that these results may not be easily generalizable to the national level."；2026_Zhuang "Consequently, caution is warranted when generalizing these findings to other cities, and further cross-city research is necessary to enhance the generalizability of the conclusions."
- **未来方向句式**："From the findings of this study, some promising directions for future research can be identified. First, further studies need to examine..."（2017_Tan）；"For future research, it would be worthwhile to conduct activity-travel diary surveys over multiple days, covering both workdays and weekends to provide a more comprehensive picture."（Tao2020）；"An interesting topic in future research is ... Will there be only minor changes, or new patterns has emerged?"（2023_Shi，问句收尾）；"These problems are being addressed in our ongoing research using cell phone location data."（2021_Lu）；"It would, of course, be an interesting question to examine ... This research challenge will be taken on board as the next stage of our investigation."（Toger2023）；"We hope our work will inspire other researchers to engage in a more nuanced and diverse exploration of travel behavior and time stress using the time crunch index."（Kim2024）；"Further research is still necessary to understand the forces that lead urban, low-income residents toward the reverse commute."（2018_Davidson）

### 2.5 收尾句（你的重点）

真实收尾句不写"总之本文研究了 X"这种无信息总结，而是**论断式/升华式/号召式/愿景式**四选一：
- **论断式**（把结果升华为一个命题）："The mismatch of job and housing in Guangzhou is more than a purely spatial problem; it is also an institutional one."（2017__6166）
- **跨学科升华**："This is not just an issue pertinent for transport planners, but across all disciplines aiming to reduce inequalities and improve well-being in society."（AllenFarber2020）
- **号召式**："It is time to consider the needs and aspirations of all workers in the 24 hour economy."（Palm2025）
- **公平愿景**："By doing so, planners and policymakers can design interventions that restore connectivity without undermining local quality of life, ensuring that the benefits of transportation infrastructure are shared equitably across all communities these roads traverse."（2026_Won_8833）
- **价值定位**："This study's relevance lies in its concern for equity among citizens identifying a gap that needs to be bridged by policy to promote the social sustainability of the city in the future."（2021_Jang_7876）
- **谦逊开放**："In conclusion, while this study significantly advances our understanding of non-work-related urban travels, ample opportunities remain for further exploration and methodological refinement."（2025_Azari_8061）

**写作判断**：收尾句必须回扣全文最大的那个 claim。你的论文可写"以核心变量为独立维度的活动空间分异框架，为时间-空间耦合的城市不平等研究提供了新的出发点"这类论断+愿景句，但每个词都要有正文支撑。

## 三、句式与例子（真实提取，按用途取用，附出处）

**RQ 收束句**：
- `To conclude, <A> and <B> are differently related to <C> for <组1> and <组2>.`（2016_Tammaru_8475）中文说明：To conclude 直接回答"关系是什么+对谁"，结论的最短收束形态。
- `We conclude that <关键机制>, i.e. <机制1> and <机制2>, have led to an independent and significant influence of <X> on <Y> when compared with <对照组>.`（2017_Tan_6973）中文说明：We conclude + i.e. 展开机制，答案=机制清单。
- `By combining <测量1> and <测量2>, this paper indicates that <核心判断> exists in both <维度A> and <维度B>.`（Ta2021）中文说明：方法组合→核心判断，一句话完成"怎么做+答案"。
- `Altogether, these findings demonstrate that <X> are not a homogeneous category but instead <细分结论>.`（2026_Zhuang_8043）中文说明：Altogether 总收 + 反同质化判断，适合"分类/分异"类研究——你的分组结论可直接用此骨架。

**贡献声明句**：
- `This paper adds to the robust body of <领域> through <做了什么>.`（2018_Davidson_6227）中文说明：adds to the robust body of——承认文献强壮，自己再加一块。
- `This research seeks to contribute to the existing literature on <领域> by <做法>, which can provide a useful reference for related research.`（2020_Wang_8696）中文说明：seeks to + by 做法，克制谦逊。
- `Drawing upon <理论>, this study contributes to extending the existing literature on <主题> from <N> aspects.`（2019_Zhang_7717）中文说明：理论+扩展文献，你（时间地理学视角）可直接套用。
- `These findings suggest that <新视角> provides a helpful perspective for achieving a deeper understanding of <对象> through focusing on <动态本质>.`（2017_Tan_6973）中文说明：视角贡献的标准句式——新视角+聚焦点。
- `In this paper, we focused on <新维度> as a crucial facet of <领域>, showing how its understanding is an increasingly important prerequisite for the planning of <目标>.`（Vitrano2023）中文说明：维度聚焦贡献+规划前提，与你"核心变量维度"最接近的真实例句。
- `Overall, this study offers a new analytical framework for understanding <关系>.`（2026_Fan_8053）中文说明：新框架贡献，一句话。
- `Based on these findings, our study makes contributions at methodological, theoretical, and applied levels. <Methodologically...> <Theoretically...> <Practically...>`（2026_Liu_7724）中文说明：三层贡献展开，每个层面一个动词短语。
- `This is a new contribution of this study to the understanding of <主题>, compared with those using <替代数据/尺度>.`（2014_Wu_6549）中文说明：与替代方案对比凸显贡献，适用于"用更细数据/新视角重审旧议题"。
- `Notwithstanding these results, it is important to stress that <核心概念> are just one dimension of <上位框架>.`（Kim2024）中文说明：贡献后的边界声明，防止单维度 overclaim。

**政策句**：
- `<Actors> should support <主张>, especially <资源> for <最脆弱群体>.`（2020_Wang_8696）中文说明：行动者+资源+受益群体三段式，政策对象=结果里的最脆弱群体。
- `This study provides support for further guiding the equitable distribution of <资源> to create <目标状态> for different <群体>.`（2015_Zhou_7343）中文说明：provide support 弱化动词，适合"证据支持方向"而非"命令"。
- `These insights provide empirical evidence to support <政策列表> aimed at improving <目标1> and promoting <目标2>.`（Zheng2026）中文说明：结果→政策列表→价值目标，时间/工作类论文（你的主题）的标准落点。
- `Given the significant share of <人群> <情境>, it is time for <领域> to <行动>.`（Palm2025）中文说明：以人群规模立论的政策号召。
- `Under resource constraints, a more effective strategy is to first <第一步> and, on this basis, prioritize <第二步>.`（2026_Wang_8274）中文说明：资源约束下的策略序列，比单句建议更具体。
- `Policy around <问题> should not only focus on <方案A>, but also on <方案B>. It is necessary to develop a means to <目标>.`（2018_Davidson_6227）中文说明：not only...but also 扩展政策视野 + It is necessary 给出机制性要求。
- `With respect to policy implications, it is important to undertake further research to explore whether <群体> face greater disadvantage in <维度>.`（2017_Tan_6973）中文说明：证据不足时的研究导向政策句——不说"应该做什么"，说"还需要研究什么"。

**局限句**：
- `We acknowledge that this study has limitations that warrant future research. Firstly, <局限1>. Secondly, <局限2>. In addition, <未来方向>.`（2024_Chen_5974）中文说明：主动承认+编号逐条，最稳的局限段框架。
- `Inevitably, this study has some limitations. First, <局限1>... In the subsequent study, it is crucial to <改进>.`（2023_Liu_8071）中文说明：每条局限紧跟改进方案。
- `It is important to note, however, that these results may not be easily generalizable to <范围>.`（2018_Davidson_6227）中文说明：外推性警告独立成句。

**未来研究句**：
- `From the findings of this study, some promising directions for future research can be identified. First, further studies need to examine <问题1>. Another important theme for future studies is <问题2>.`（2017_Tan_6973）中文说明：总起+First+Another 双条目。
- `For future research, it would be worthwhile to <方案>, which will provide a more comprehensive picture of <概念>.`（Tao2020）中文说明：would be worthwhile 是最常见的建议式。
- `An interesting topic in future research is <方向>. Will there be only minor changes, or new patterns has emerged?`（2023_Shi_7664）中文说明：以问句收未来，语气开放。
- `These problems are being addressed in our ongoing research using <数据>.`（2021_Lu_5979）中文说明：把未来研究变成"已在路上"。
- `We hope our work will inspire other researchers to engage in a more nuanced and diverse exploration of <议题> using <工具>.`（Kim2024）中文说明：呼吁式未来句。

**收尾句**：
- `The mismatch of <X> and <Y> in <城市> is more than a purely spatial problem; it is also an institutional one.`（2017__6166）中文说明：论断式收尾——"不只是 X，也是 Y"升维。
- `This is not just an issue pertinent for <领域>, but across all disciplines aiming to reduce <不平等> and improve <福祉>.`（AllenFarber2020）中文说明：跨学科升华，把议题拔高。
- `It is time to consider the needs and aspirations of <全体对象> in <时代>.`（Palm2025）中文说明：号召式，给全体对象赋权。
- `By doing so, <行动者> can design interventions that <目标>, ensuring that the benefits of <基础设施> are shared equitably across <受影响群体>.`（2026_Won_8833）中文说明：公平愿景式收尾，行动者+受益者齐全。
- `As <趋势> continue to evolve, <主体> will need to balance <A> with <B>; this study offers an early roadmap for <导航> and <准备>.`（2026_Chamberlain_8947）中文说明：前瞻蓝图式——给未来行动者一张地图。
- `Ultimately, building on this study's findings can help develop <更宏大的知识目标>.`（2026_Ding_7711）中文说明：理论愿景式收尾。

## 四、陷阱与红线（真实审稿/批注教训）

1. **❌ 结论=摘要复读**：整段复制摘要的发现句，不新增贡献/政策/边界。真实结论即使复述发现也换角度（机制、理论、政策），并加新的收束（2016_Wang 首句 "Public housing residents in Hong Kong are disadvantaged in many respects" 是对摘要发现的重新定性）。允许核心句逐字呼应（2017_Tan、2021_Lu），禁止整段复制。
2. **❌ 只总结不升华**：结论只有发现复述，没有贡献/政策/边界任何一个。2016_Tammaru 的 "Overall, these results confirm that it is important to go beyond residential segregation and conceptualize segregation across multiple interlinked domains (Marcinczak et al., 2015; ...)" 是把发现升华到理论。
3. **❌ 贡献夸大无支撑**：真实贡献句都有"具体做法/对象"支撑（by portraying a more realistic picture / through building typologies / by quantifying...），动词克制（adds to / contributes to / offers / enriches）；"This is the first paper" 只在真实首创论文出现（Kim2024、2023_Oostenbach），你的论文不用。
4. **❌ 政策建议无行动者、无受益群体**：真实政策句都有 who（social policies / planners / it is time for transportation research and planning）与 for whom（vulnerable groups / low-income individuals relocated to the suburbs）。2020_Wang 是范本。
5. **❌ 政策句与讨论政策段重复**：合并时结论给"落点清单"，讨论给"论证"；同一政策不在两处展开两遍。
6. **❌ 局限与未来脱节**：局限说"数据不足"却不说"未来怎么办"。真实局限每条都带未来方案（2023_Liu、2026_Lee、2020_Wang）。
7. **❌ 结论引入新发现/新引用/新术语**：真实结论不复述新数字（DongMiller2026 被标注为"结论不再复述数字，只上升为概括性命题"）、不引入新文献；新概念标签（2026_Won "behavioral disconnection"）在前文定义过，结论只回收。
8. **❌ 收尾句软塌**："In conclusion, this study investigated X" 没有信息。真实收尾是论断式/升华式/号召式/愿景式（2.5）。
9. **❌ 局限段含糊其辞**：真实局限都是主动承认（We acknowledge / This study still has some limitations / Several limitations point to clear next steps），不是 "Some may argue"。
10. **❌ 元统计堆砌**：不写"数十篇里 34 篇五步走"这种话（本指南仅供写作参考，论文正文绝不出现元统计）。
